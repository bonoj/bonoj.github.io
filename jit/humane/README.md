# HUMANE — House of Provenance

## Thesis
HUMANE is an immaculate heritage butcher and luxury marketplace for individually sourced human organs. The institution speaks in the serene, precise language of artisanal sourcing, traceability, exceptional cuts, and care. Donor biography is product provenance. The ambiguity between transplant, consumption, and collectible luxury remains unresolved.

## Art direction
Parchment, cotton paper, unbleached ivory, oxblood ink, walnut, delicate rules, archival serif typography, kraft labels, linen string and twine. Airy, immaculate, editorial. Product still-life photography should evoke fine butchery, not splatter or shock. No comedy voice, meta language, arrows-as-link-icons, badges saying fictional, or out-of-character notices on the public experience.

## Experience
Single-page responsive boutique with editorial hero, provenance philosophy, searchable/filterable six-product catalog, donor-specific stories, product detail modal, functional local shopping basket and quantities, no actual checkout or payment. Browse by Hearts, Livers, Kidneys, and Rare Cuts. Cart is ephemeral in memory. No user information is requested or transmitted. Every product gets a human history, place, age, habits, a note of personal life, preparation, price and lot number. Interaction should work on mobile and desktop with keyboard-accessible controls.
