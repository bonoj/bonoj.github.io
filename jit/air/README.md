# AIR. MORE THAN EVER.

**Live:** https://bonoj.github.io/jit/air/

A fictional American commodity-awareness campaign for air, presented with the solemn institutional confidence of a national advertising initiative. The site itself never breaks character.

## Premise

The American Air Council has dedicated itself to ensuring that Americans continue to recognize and use air. The institution treats universal awareness and ubiquitous availability as achievements of its own marketing programs, while asserting there is still room to grow.

Core campaign line: **AIR. MORE THAN EVER.™**

## Experience

- Full-bleed landscape hero and cinematic lifestyle photography.
- Editorial introduction and three photographic campaign pillars.
- The State of Air: 100% awareness, 24/7 availability, and first place among breathing essentials.
- National Air Awareness Tracker at 100% with a call to help reach 100%.
- Testimonial from Jennifer M. in Ohio and a science section celebrating the relationship between air and breathing.
- Three programs: The Next Generation of Breathing, Air at Work, and The Air Advantage.
- Air Pledge: local, no-data, checkbox-confirmation interaction; no registration or external submission.
- Institutional governance section and solemn closing campaign statement.

## Technical contract

Standalone `index.html`, responsive for mobile and desktop, no build process, backend, external JS framework, user tracking, or personal data collection. Fonts load from Google Fonts with system fallbacks; photography loads from Unsplash image CDN. If external services are unavailable, images may not load.

## Art direction

Genuinely luxurious and completely earnest. The humor comes from an institution celebrating the obvious and inventing demand for something universal. No winking copy, obvious joke graphics, or parody disclaimers on the public-facing site. This README records the fictional nature of the project.
