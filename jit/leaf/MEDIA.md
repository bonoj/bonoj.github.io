# Leaf — Visual reference and media manifest

Status: **discovery, not downloaded or embedded**. Image search results are candidate source pages; each chosen image needs its actual file URL and placement/crop inspection. The earlier in-chat banana-tree photographs are the preferred aesthetic, but their exact original URLs are not established in this workspace.

## Direction
Mature banana plants; heavy arched midribs, split drooping blades, wind-torn edges, luminous translucent green. Editorial photography with a sense of scale. Side-on leaf silhouette, not top-down botanical diagram. No water-droplet logo gimmick.

## Concrete source leads
1. Wikimedia Commons banana-leaves category — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Banana_leaves — broad candidate gallery, including `Banana leaf after monsoon.jpg`, `Banana Leaves India.jpg`, `Banana tree leafs.jpg`, `Banana Palm & Light Torquay (15583544735).jpg`, `Banana Leaf + Sun 5.jpg`. **Candidate filenames only**; inspect individual photographs before assigning slots.
2. Wikimedia Commons `Banana leaf closeup.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Banana_leaf_closeup.jpg — source page lists photographer Akash.lumos and original dimensions 2304×4096. Potential DETAIL-01; inspect for composition and exact reuse terms.
3. USF Digital Commons `Row of Banana Trees with Large Leaves` — https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/wagner/357/ — Francis G. Wagner photograph, circa 1920, archival monochrome; potential About/Newsroom historical editorial, not a green hero.
