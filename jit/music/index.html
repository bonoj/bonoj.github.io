EXPERIMENT 006 · COMPOSITION AS GROWTH

The Musical Tree

A motif is a seed. Each branch inherits its parent's music and changes it. Select a phrase; grow, transform, or prune it. Listen to the consequences.

Seed selected · one phrase

Each node is a phrase, played from left to right through the tree. Grow appends a related variation. Transform changes the selected phrase. Prune removes its whole branch. Maximum 12 phrases.

EXPERIMENT 007 · CONTINUOUS VIBRATING STRINGS

Three Spectra

Three persistent voices, not a sequence of notes. Touch to excite. Drag horizontally to slide continuously along the pitch spectrum. Tap higher or lower than the glowing marker to hammer-on or pull-off.

Sleeping · touch Awaken to unlock sound

Select a string by touching its track. Hammer and pull modify its current pitch and excitation. Mute damps it. Let go and listen to the decay; there is no note sequencer.

EXPERIMENT 008 · TOUCH INSTRUMENT

D–A–F · open D minor. Hold a fret on the neck with one finger; sweep another finger across the soundboard to strum. Slide a held finger to hammer-on or pull-off.

Ready · unlock audio, then fret and strum

Neck: D3 · A3 · F4, each with eight diatonic positions (0–12 semitones). Soundboard: drag vertically across the strings. Two fingers work independently.
