# STC image provenance and editorial selection

Images are real documentary records used as contextual editorial material for a **fictional** institution. Depicted individuals, photographers, governments, charities and organizations are **not affiliated with STC** and do not endorse its claims. The STC portfolio figures and programs are invented. Never identify pictured children as recruits or pictured civilians as supporters of violent groups.

## Rights and representation

The Wikimedia Commons file pages are the authoritative sources for attribution and license conditions. CC BY-SA images may trigger share-alike obligations for adapted images. Color grading/cropping can count as adaptation; check requirements. A Creative Commons license does not authorize misleading claims of endorsement or affiliation. Editorial use beside STC's fictional practices must not imply that real victims, children, communities, aid workers or photographers are participants in those practices.
