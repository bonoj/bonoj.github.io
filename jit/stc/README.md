# STC Capital — The Future of Human Need

Standalone JIT: https://bonoj.github.io/jit/stc/

## Creative contract
Fictional institutional satire, presented in an entirely deadpan corporate voice. The public-facing experience does not wink at the audience or explain the joke. It examines how polished institutional language can normalize profiteering from famine, conflict, fundamentalism, child recruitment, dependency and reconstruction.

## Site architecture
- Institutional landing page: worldview, six sector practices, impact reporting, conversion.
- Primary conversion: **Request the Prospectus**. Opens an in-browser, five-chapter editorial document, with direct-link, copy-link and print functionality. No investment portal, login, payments or lead capture.
- Secondary journey: **Explore our work**.
- Single self-contained `index.html`, responsive styles, no build pipeline.

## Editorial boundaries
All organizational claims, KPIs and purported strategies are invented for the satirical institution. They are not real-world measurements, financial offerings or accusations about any named real organization. The site does not collect money, personal data or email addresses. Current photos are generic remote Unsplash images, not verified documentary evidence of the portrayed practices. Do not represent real individuals as affiliated with STC. If replacing imagery, maintain an internal provenance ledger and verify reuse rights.
