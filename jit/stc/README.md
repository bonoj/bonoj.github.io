## Intent
STC Capital is a fictional institution that speaks in sterile, self-serious impact-investment language. Its institutional worldview treats persistent human need as an opportunity for recurring financial returns. It does not claim affiliation with real institutions. The public homepage and investor portal form a single satirical experience; the corporate voice never breaks character.

## Current implementation
- Editorial corporate homepage, forest green / ivory identity, sector cards and photography.
- Investor portal with six fictional mandates, five real-world geographic labels, $10K simulated allocations, quarterly progression, ROI, welfare, dependency, schooling, social stability, and event log.
- No payment collection. No real investments. Regional indicators and outcomes are **simulated**, not live datasets. Photo assets are external Unsplash links, illustrative and not geographically verified.
- Research links: World Bank Open Data, UNDP, UNICEF, Wikimedia Commons. These are sources for context, **not STC partners**.
- Future direction: source-verified documentary imagery, region-specific public datasets, deeper coupled community systems, and auditably separated historical observations vs fictional scenario effects.
