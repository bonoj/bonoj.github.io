# I. INVITATION TO THE GRAND OPENING

Welcome to EVERMORE GAMES, a completely fictional, splendidly overfunded mobile gaming empire. Every title is breathtakingly familiar. Every decision is correct. Every tap is rewarded. Every economy is simultaneously overflowing with wealth and experiencing a critical shortage of one more currency.

The flagship, **MATCH 1™**, removes the inconvenient requirement to match three things. One tap suffices. Its sequel, **MATCH 0™**, has removed the tap as well. Our shareholders regard this as a breakthrough in consumer convenience. Our scientists have established that a 100% success rate can be improved by 15% without altering it.

This is **beautifully banal** by design: gorgeous jewel-toned mobile-game language, glossy prizes, gold-trimmed UI, triumphant animations, frictionless fictional progression, and an ever-expanding structure of rewards for rewards for rewards. The comedy lies in systems that take their own nonsense with impeccable seriousness. There are **no real-money purchases, payment processing, genuine investment products, or actual gambling**. The satirical economy is local to the browser.

**Ceremonial proclamation:** The player is our most valued customer, shareholder, monarch, student, taxpayer, particle employer, defendant, creditor, and entirely voluntary subject. Tiffany thanks you for your service.

## Flagship: MATCH 1™

**Player action:** tap a jewel. **System response:** victory, score, currency rewards, praise, particle spectacle. Its deliberately trivial input is the seed of the entire interinstitutional chain: a match can create stars and coins; rewards can become ledger events; events can move securities and research; celebrations can manufacture particle records; particles can be reclaimed and taxed; legal disputes can generate still more ceremonies. The original puzzle is the smallest possible ignition switch for the largest possible bureaucracy.

**Lore:** In the first age, citizens matched three gems. In the second age, they matched one. In the promised third age, matching will be prohibited as unnecessary user friction. A university department is investigating whether zero taps constitute a negative tap.

**Future:** gemstone botanical classification; 128-stage victory combos; royal sponsorship of individual gems; gem-specific unions; gem ancestry; a competitive ranking system in which all players share first place; a spectator mode that congratulates observers for watching.

## Kingdom Eternal

The royal realm contains buildings, upgrades, and civic pride. Its rulers are subject to planning permits, appreciation assessments, royal builder-protection law, and competing claims from Tiffany's cabinet. Future building permits should feed Parliament, taxes, securities, and the cross-game supply chain.

## Faction Wars

Conflict is guaranteed to produce glory. Every faction may be sovereign, but all factions ultimately belong to the same parent company. Future diplomacy will allow sanctions on one's own kingdoms, followed by humanitarian relief paid from the same treasury.

## Merge Forever

Dragon eggs are both pets and speculative financial instruments. Every hatch could create a genetic record, a new royal claimant, a stock-market movement, and an insurance event. The Dragon Egg Index must never become less exciting merely because no dragon has actually hatched.

## Frozen Throne

Warmth is a strategic commodity. A snowstorm should influence heat production, the Weather Bureau, and the warmth securities market. Future treaties may require players to declare their winter preferences to a multinational climate commission chaired by Tiffany.

## Hero Ascension

Heroes do their own work. Players are praised for allowing it. A future Labor Ministry should classify idle heroes as both employed and on vacation, making them simultaneously eligible for wages, vacation pay, and retirement.

## Royal Renovation

The mansion is an appreciating asset and a never-ending aesthetic obligation. Every new wallpaper pattern should increase property value, trigger reassessment, and require a royal inspection.

## Treasure Tycoon

Wealth accumulates because accumulation is its own justification. This is the natural home for stock exchange settlement, Royal Reserve audits, sovereign wealth bonds, and increasingly intricate fictional bookkeeping.

## Island Paradise

Islands produce resources, which justify boats, which justify customs, which justify international maritime law, which justifies an entirely new currency. The future Island Customs Authority must be a delightfully inefficient branch of the shared logistics system.

## Galaxy Dominion

Space is enormous, but paperwork scales better. Planets may declare independence, be acquired by Evermore Parent Holdings, and continue to receive the same Tiffany tutorial. Future interstellar settlement can take place in Tomorrow Tokens.

## Pet Champions

Affection is both a feeling and a financial instrument. Companion Bonds, Friendship, Loyalty, Admiration, and Tiffany Favor are all plausible denominations in a civilization that insists on measuring devotion.

## Match 0

A sequel that has won before opening. It is the ideal site for retroactive achievements, Yesterday Credits, paradox tax, and a Causality Tribunal. **Its highest possible victory animation should play before the user arrives.**

# IV. THE EVERMORE SHARED GRAMMAR

1. **One empire, twelve doors.** The catalog is not a collection of unrelated prototypes; it is one corporate identity with a consistent language.
2. **Instant legibility.** A player should understand the immediate action without reading a manual.
3. **Victory is the default.** Friction is largely ceremonial. The joke is overabundance, not cruelty.
4. **Every reward has provenance.** Currency, event, achievement, and celebration should eventually be traceable to a concrete action.
5. **Every screen is a department.** Distinct thematic flavor, shared visual vocabulary.
6. **Every institution creates dependencies.** A new system should interpret old events, not merely sit beside them.
7. **Tiffany is everywhere.** Tutorial guide, finance minister, monarch, university chancellor, wedding officiant, judge, and institutional continuity.
8. **Local, fictional, reversible.** No real purchases, personal financial data, external accounts, or manipulative real-world scarcity.
9. **Mobile first.** Comfortable thumb targets, single-column fallbacks, scrollable horizontal navigation, no page-level sideways overflow.
10. **Particles are serious business.** Spectacle has an economy, and the economy generates more spectacle.

## Visual bible

Royal violet and midnight plum; warm gold and ivory; jewel-pink, diamond-blue, mint, and magical lavender accents. Rounded, glossy, densely rewarding mobile interfaces. Gradient panels, small ornate labels, decorative badges, celebratory rings, jewel icons, confetti, and unabashed exclamation marks. The visual language should be *immaculately consistent*, never deliberately ugly. Satire is strongest when the product looks expensive and entirely convinced of itself.

# V. THE GRAND ECONOMIC ENCYCLOPEDIA

## Founding wallet

The initial shared progression includes **Coins, Diamonds, Stars, Energy, Crowns, and Tickets**. Other specialized quantities and prestige instruments arrive as the empire expands. These balances are fictional and persisted locally. Some are universal; others are subsystem-specific.

## Six reserve families, eight denominations each

### 👑 Royal Tender
**Crowns · Lesser Crowns · Crown Shards · Crown Dust · Royal Seals · Sovereign Marks · Imperial Pennies · Crown Futures.**

Royal Tender is the official language of monarchy, land, ceremony, and fiscal legitimacy. Crown Dust is the inevitable result of repeatedly dividing a perfectly good Crown. Crown Futures permit citizens to be optimistic about crowns they have not yet earned.

### 🔮 Arcane Capital
**Mana · Greater Mana · Mana Shards · Ether · Ether Residue · Spellmarks · Mystic Sparks · Unspent Potential.**

Arcane Capital prices the impossible. Unspent Potential is the most philosophically honest asset in the realm, because its value increases the longer nobody does anything with it.

### ⚔️ Martial Assets
**Valor · Honor · Glory · Battle Marks · Victory Fragments · Heroic Essence · Campaign Seals · Tactical Prestige.**

These are awarded for heroic deeds, strategic participation, and the ability to win battles with no possibility of defeat. A Victory Fragment is not evidence of a partial victory; it is a whole victory subdivided for accounting convenience.

### 💖 Affection Instruments
**Hearts · Friendship · Loyalty · Admiration · Devotion · Companion Bonds · Warmth · Tiffany Favor.**

The realm's most emotionally liquid market. Companion Bonds are simultaneously evidence of love and an investment product. Tiffany Favor is the benchmark asset against which all other feelings are measured.

### ⏳ Temporal Securities
**Seconds · Saved Seconds · Bonus Minutes · Time Shards · Chrono Dust · Tomorrow Tokens · Yesterday Credits · Pending Eternities.**

Time is both a resource and an accounting problem. Yesterday Credits are suitable for refunds issued in prior fiscal years. Pending Eternities mature eventually, in a manner of speaking.

### 🌟 Prestige Derivatives
**Prestige · Greater Prestige · Prestige Shards · Legacy · Ascension · Transcendence · Rebirth Credits · Prestige Prestige.**

Prestige Prestige is what happens when an empire runs out of meaningful ways to say Prestige. This is not a defect; it is a roadmap.

**Total:** 48 reserve denominations, in addition to the founding wallet and subsequent specialist credits. Conversion uses fictional reference rates and a ceremonial spread. The Royal Reserve Bank supports vault inspection, grants, exchange, conversion, accounts, audits, receipts, and Tiffany's advice.

## The Royal Reserve Bank

The bank exists to transform simple rewards into ornate financial obligations. Central-bank grants distribute multiple denominations. Audits certify financial magnificence. Exchanges impose spreads. The account ledger remembers transactions. A user can be both wealthy and in need of an immediate emergency liquidity celebration.

## Department of Rewards

Rewards generate receipts. Receipts generate claimable rewards. Claims generate new paperwork and further Prestige Shards. The receipt is the unit of bureaucratic reproduction. In a mature economy, the receipt may become more valuable than the event it records.

## Everything Factory and Universal Supply Chain

The Great Convergence established a cross-game supply-chain presentation, universal objectives, factory production, and an exchange. Its initial stages include ceremonial advancement; **not all supply-chain stages are automatically driven by underlying game actions**. The roadmap must connect actual production, construction, battles, pet bonds, and planetary actions to stage completion rather than pretending this wiring is finished.

## Stock Exchange

Eight fictional listed securities: Royal Keep Futures; Dragon Egg Holdings; Tiffany Favor Bonds; Match 1 Victory Fund; Frozen Warmth Energy; Heroic Inaction Group; Galactic Real Estate; Evermore Parent Holdings. Users buy and sell using fictional coins; holdings and prices persist. Gameplay and institutional events can affect prices. Dividends are payable. **This is a simplified game mechanic, not a realistic order-book exchange.**

# VI. THE AGE OF INSTITUTIONS

## Royal Marriage Market

Every wedding joins two named nobles, creates a dynasty, appoints an heir a duke, and yields tax consequences and companion-bond rewards. Dynastic entries persist. The present generator is intentionally compact; full genealogical genetics, intergenerational marriages, legal succession, and family trees belong to the roadmap.

## Evermore Revenue Service

Form 1040-MORE guarantees the player has overpaid. Refunds arrive in Yesterday Credits. Tax credits accrue from selected events. The system records filings and refunds, but a comprehensive progressive tax engine spanning every denomination is future work. The institution's moral principle is simple: refunds are taxable, but the refund tax is refundable.

## Parliament

Twenty-four seats represent twelve game-worlds. Citizens vote on proposed laws. Every proposal passes 24–0 regardless of the citizen's vote, which is nevertheless thanked for its meaningful participation. Enacted legislation persists in the statute book. The first bills concern gem classification, builders, Tiffany, dragon egg officeholders, financial clarity, and victory rights. **Broad mechanical effects of each law are still an expansion opportunity.**

## Evermore University

Research Knowledge is earned from institutional events. Eight programs consume it: Advanced Gem Recognition, Accelerated Construction, Dragon Appreciation, Economics of More, Tiffany Studies, Temporal Efficiency, Applied Democracy, and Applied Victory Studies. Research ranks persist. The doctorate is awarded for pursuing a field in which failure is impossible. Research currently functions mainly as progression and ceremony; more systemic consequences are planned.

## Department of Existential Necessity

The department reviews proposed features. All are essential. Every approval generates additional administrative requirements. Its growing registry is a monument to the impossibility of ever being finished. The logical end state is an audit of the department itself, which will be unanimously approved.

## Tiffany's Office of Universal Governance

Tiffany serves as Prime Minister, central banker, chief justice, university chancellor, wedding officiant, and CEO. Royal decrees can affect specific balances and create event records; some effects are narrative. The office's constitutional position is unambiguous: Tiffany is accountable to Parliament, whose representatives were appointed by Tiffany.

## Universal institutional event stream

An inspectable, locally persisted sequence connects certain game rewards and institutional actions to market prices, research knowledge, tax credits, and other balances. The event log is bounded for mobile storage. **Not every action in every game has yet been connected; this is the critical integration frontier.**

# VII. PARTICLE INDUSTRIALIZATION

## The Festival

The particle engine creates contextual bursts, emoji and glowing dots, confetti ribbons, expanding rings, and rain. Match taps, bank activity, ceremonies, weddings, votes, research, and decrees are among its triggers. DOM particles are bounded and removed after their animations; reduced-motion preferences are respected. A mobile device should experience excess without retaining thousands of permanent nodes.

## Six Foundries

Sparkles, Glitters, Twinkles, Shimmers, Confetti, and Premium Confetti have their own production factories. Each supports factory-level upgrades and unionized worker hiring, both using fictional coins. Manufacturing creates aggregate output, an event in the registry, and a corresponding celebration. Premium Confetti is indistinguishable from ordinary confetti; its superiority is contractual.

## Ministry of Reclamation

Produced particles can be reclaimed into Dust. Dust refines into Essence. Essence mints Particle Credits. Each transformation produces its own celebratory particle event, ensuring the ministry can never entirely reduce the supply of particles. This is **the Reclamation Paradox**.

## Royal Weather Bureau

Ordinary Sunshine, Glitter Storm, Gold Rain, Diamond Hail, Heart Showers, and Bureaucratic Fog appear as forecast states. Weather events generate visual effects and production records. The forecast is 100% confident and 0% accurate. **True gameplay-wide weather modifiers remain planned, not shipped.**

## Particle Rights Tribunal

A representative particle can bring suit for premature despawning, unpaid glitter overtime, unlawful recycling, inheritance, or misclassification. Tiffany adjudicates. Cases and outcomes are recorded. The tribunal is currently a satirical procedural generator, not an adversarial simulation.

## Great Particle Census

Every production event increases aggregate counts and creates a **representative named registry entry**: royal title, serial, birthplace/source, dependents, and tragic lifespan. The system does **not** store an individual permanent record for each ephemeral rendered DOM particle; this distinction is intentional for mobile performance. Representative records are bounded while aggregate counts persist.

## Retirement System

Particle Credits fund the pension system. Retirement age: 65 years. Expected particle lifespan: 1.4 seconds. The fund's theoretical solvency is therefore extraordinary. Tiffany considers the absence of living claimants a testament to excellent governance.

# VIII. LORE BIBLE: THE TIFFANY DOCTRINE

**Tiffany is not a mascot who appears in every game. Tiffany is the only truly persistent institution in Evermore.**

She changes wardrobe and title, but never conviction. She believes that all actions are victories, all systems are essential, all financial positions are strong, all legislation is popular, all scientific discoveries are profound, and all particles have enjoyed full lives. She is never knowingly cruel. She is genuinely, almost cosmically delighted.

3. **The Banker:** Your balance is magnificent, especially after conversion fees!

### Canonical corporate laws

- Every game is the flagship of its own market segment.

- Every victory requires a ceremony.

# XII. ENGINEERING & OPERATIONS CONTRACT

## Current architecture

A self-contained static HTML/CSS/JS experience served through GitHub Pages, with a shared global state and browser-local persistence. It is a fast experimental JIT website, not a production multiplayer service. The original UI and successive feature phases have been added incrementally, and the resulting script contains several layers of wrapped render, navigation, reward and celebration functions.

**Strengths:** extremely rapid iteration; one deployable file; no server; no authentication; immediate mobile access; a shared state and visual grammar; lightweight particle rendering.

**Risks:** growing single-file complexity; function wrapping and ordering dependencies; broad mutable global state; accidental duplicate effects; incomplete cross-game event wiring; limited automated testing; browser-local data loss; performance cost from large DOM updates and repeated particle events.

## Prioritized technical debt

1. **Regression test the entire navigation tree**, including home, catalog, all twelve games, bank, government, particle industry, and hash navigation after refresh.
2. **Verify bank denomination identifiers** and every conversion/grant/receipt path; protect against missing state fields and malformed older saves.
3. **Consolidate event emission** so one gameplay action produces one authoritative semantic event and downstream institutions subscribe to it.
4. **Avoid duplicate particle accounting** when a ceremony wraps an action that already produces particles.
5. **Make economic conservation explicit:** define issuance, sinks, conversion spread, minting, reclamation, and stock trades.
6. **Separate visual particles from economic particles:** distinguish sampled display events, manufactured quantities, and named registry representatives.
7. **Protect mobile performance:** bounded particle count, bounded event history, responsive layouts, 44px-ish tap targets, and no unintended horizontal scrolling.
8. **Harden persistence:** versioned save migrations, export/import, recovery from corrupted localStorage, and reset confirmation.
9. **Add deterministic tests** for financial arithmetic, event propagation, dynasty creation, taxes, legislation, and particle production.
10. **Document feature flags and unfinished wiring** instead of silently representing narrative effects as implemented mechanics.
11. **Audit accessibility:** keyboard focus, contrast, reduced motion, readable currency numbers, and announcements for important results.
12. **Create a single institution registry** rather than perpetually stacking unrelated function wrappers.

## Suggested event envelope

```js
{
  id: "evt-00018472",
  time: "2026-10-14T01:15:36Z",
  source: "match1",
  type: "victory.completed",
  actor: "local-player",
  payload: { gems: 1, stars: 1, coins: 75 },
  causationId: "tap-00018471",
  version: 1
}
```

Proposed processing pipeline:

```text
Player action
    ↓
Authoritative gameplay event
    ├── Shared wallet / inventory
    ├── Supply chain / factories
    ├── Bank / monetary accounting
    ├── Stock market / dividends
    ├── Parliament / laws / taxes
    ├── University / research
    ├── Dynasties / inheritance
    ├── Particle economy / registry
    ├── News / achievements / museum
    └── Presentation / ceremony / visual particles
```

**Key principle:** semantic causality first, spectacle second. The same event can be interpreted by twelve systems without being counted twelve times as the original action.

## Deployment discipline

Before JIT delivery, read **[Gate](https://github.com/bonoj/Gate/blob/main/README.md)**. Inspect actual repository state, preserve existing systems, use established incremental write/update routes, read back commits, check GitHub Actions, and verify the public bytes before claiming a release is live. The canonical public URL is https://bonoj.github.io/jit/evermore/ . The build's Git commit is the historical identity; a query-string label is not proof of deployment.

## Definition of a successful future phase

- It is genuinely navigable and usable on a phone.
- Its feature claims are accurate.
- It persists where appropriate.
- It emits or consumes at least one meaningful shared event.
- It has a satirical identity and coherent Tiffany involvement.
- It creates delightful visual consequences.
- It does not silently break any earlier game or institution.
- The source, commit, deployment status and limitations are inspectable.

The initial IPO/FX script contained **two unescaped single quotes** inside generated HTML button handlers. Those quotes broke parsing of the entire IPO/FX JavaScript block. **What passed:** source commit, source readback, all script syntax checks, and static handler-reference inspection.

**What was NOT established:** live Pages delivery, real browser interaction, exhaustive gameplay tests, or successful responses from the external FX APIs. **Never describe these as passed until actually tested.**

**New release invariant:** Before committing or publishing an Evermore update, parse **every inline script**, inventory and inspect event-handler targets, check all route registrations and deep links, then read back the committed source. After publishing, inspect the Pages workflow and public bytes; verify at least one interaction in each materially changed system. A commit is not a live build. A passing parser is not a passing game.
