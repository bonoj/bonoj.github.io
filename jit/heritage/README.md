# HERITAGE — Other People's Provenance

## Status
SPEC ONLY. No public experience built yet. Preserve AIR, PATERNAL, HUMANE and all other JIT experiments. Publication target when built: https://bonoj.github.io/jit/heritage/.

## Thesis
HERITAGE is a flawlessly sincere luxury identity-acquisition institution. It sells OPP: Other People's Provenance. Where HUMANE merchandises bodies, HERITAGE merchandises biographies, names, distinctions, social recognition, institutional belonging, and the accumulated prestige of other people's lives. Its central proposition: **Identity is too valuable to remain the exclusive property of its original holder.** The institution never winks, apologizes, or announces its satire in the public experience. The horror arises from the composure of its administrative and commercial logic.

## Foundational distinction
Genealogy is the factual record of ancestry; pedigree is socially recognized legitimacy. HERITAGE brokers the latter, describing provenance as an alienable asset. The more believable mechanisms are introductions, patronage, affiliation, legal relationships, heraldic commissions, public narratives and recognition networks, not fabricated birth records. The business's invented legal doctrine treats identity and reputational equity as separable, transferable commodities. This is fictional institutional overreach, **not** an assertion of actual legal rights. Death does not make an estate, likeness, name or knighthood public domain; disgrace does not extinguish rights or permit identity theft. Preserve the difference in internal design while the institution's public voice confidently obscures it.

## Acquisition channels
1. **Estate Release / Obituary to Inventory** — Death treated as a liquidity event. Distinguished lives enter the acquisition queue. Biography, honors, networks, associations and visual identity are listed as components, despite the reality that estates, heirs and rights holders retain legal claims.
2. **Nullification** — HERITAGE declares a living or deceased disgraced 'bad actor' unfit to retain their provenance; a fictional internal review assigns the holder NULL status and offers their desirable credentials for reassignment. The moral outrage is that the institution arrogates authority to itself.
3. **Reinstantiation** — A premium identity reconstructed from curated fragments: achievements without scandals, social recognition without the inconvenient original life. Signature promise: 'The life you should have lived. Already lived.'
4. **Sovereign Collection** — Full house identity: heraldry, dynastic narrative, portraiture, curated ancestors, institutional affinities, succession, ceremonies and continuity. 'Why inherit a dynasty when you can commission one?'
5. **Continuity / Living Pedigree** — Not all acquisitions involve death or scandal. Families in financial decline, retired luminaries, forgotten scholars, estranged branches, institutions and patronage networks can be recast as voluntary, involuntary, or purportedly available sources. Distinguish authentic affiliation from fictitious reassignment.

## Premium packages
- **The Introduction** — introductory name, affiliation, ancestral portrait and certificate; entry-level prestige.
- **The Distinguished Life** — curated academic, artistic, civic and military narrative; supporting archival dossier.
- **Legacy Admissions** — 'Admission to the family that gets admitted.' A satire of legacy networks and institutional belonging, not a promise of university admission.
- **The First Generation** — patronage, archives, family office identity and multigenerational continuity; starting concept $2,400,000.
- **Reinstantiation Privée** — discretionary nullification, reassignment, narrative sanitization and identity portrait.
- **The Sovereign Collection** — comprehensive dynastic identity; price on application.
Pricing should be confidently exorbitant and internally coherent, not novelty-store cheap. The prices are part of the joke.

## Experience / interaction
A responsive, editorial private wealth-management website with a catalog of identities, estates, lineages and attributes; portrait-led dossiers; a provenance acquisition workflow; premium package configurator; and an interactive identity composition preview. A dossier exposes name, lifespan or status, field of distinction, institutional affiliations, original-holder status, provenance chain, availability, acquisition terms and cost. Show a curated transformation: the client selects components and sees their assembled pedigree and certificate evolve. The dramatic signature interaction is **DECLARE ORIGINAL HOLDER NULL**, followed by an institutional confirmation: 'The original holder’s contribution to this provenance has been acknowledged. Their continued association is no longer required.' Then the portrait changes to a stylized client stand-in, not a deceptive forged real-world ID. A live-feeling inventory feed may use authored fictional examples; do not claim real people died or were disgraced without verification. Do not impersonate living people or generate usable counterfeit documents.

## Information architecture
Hero ('A better past awaits.'), acquisition philosophy ('Their history. Your legacy.'), estate inventory, nullification desk, reinstantiation atelier, premium collections, identity composition experience, continuity office, institutional relations and private inquiry. Navigation and calls to action must actually work in the prototype. An authored, deterministic dataset is sufficient. Any application, cart or inquiry remains local; no payments, backend, actual verification or collection of sensitive personal data.

## Art direction
Not AIR's national campaign, PATERNAL's medical clinic, or HUMANE's artisanal butcher. HERITAGE is a discreet transnational family office / antiquarian auction house: near-black green, antique brass, oxblood wax, ivory vellum, archival portraits, embossed seals, hairline rules, generous gutters, exquisite serif typography, high-contrast editorial scale. Elegant, sober, quietly predatory. Images must be distinct and relevant to each dossier; no duplicated portrait masquerading as multiple identities. No generic corporate cards or gratuitous arrow icons. Mobile typography must be comfortably legible; sections must preserve horizontal gutters, using padding-block for vertical spacing. Keyboard and touch accessible.

## Public voice
Perfectly sincere, euphemistic, precise, possessive. No 'fictional concept', 'satire', 'parody', 'no services available', or apologetic qualifiers on the public site. Suitable lines: 'Other People's Provenance.' 'A better past awaits.' 'Their history. Your legacy.' 'Some families are born into opportunity. Others acquire it.' 'Greatness is temporary. Ownership needn't be.' 'Every enduring family begins somewhere.' 'Some names deserve a second owner.' 'EST. BEFORE YOU WERE.' These are creative copy, not legal representations.

## Safety and factual integrity
The product is satire and a local interactive fiction. No real identity transfer, forged credentials, legal documents, background-check evasion, actionable impersonation or commercial rights claims. For real figures, accurately separate documented biography from the fictitious premise; prefer composite or invented dossier subjects for the working site. Avoid asserting unverified deaths, crimes, title ownership or availability. The private README can explain the conceit; the public institution remains in character.

## Core satirical insight
HERITAGE doesn't sell titles; it sells **recognition**. Pedigree is manufactured by other people agreeing to treat it as real. The first generation pays. The second belongs. The third forgets there was a transaction.


## Experience direction — exceptional-property curation (2026-11-04)
**This section supersedes the earlier configurator-first emphasis.** The primary site experience is the curation and acquisition of exceptional *lives*, treated with the reverence, photographic richness, specificity, and salesmanship of a high-end estate agency (the user's reference is JIT Hausgeist). The identity composer becomes a secondary, post-selection experience. HERITAGE should not feel like a novelty title store, generic ecommerce catalog, or collection of credential toggles.

### Visitor journey
1. **Arrival:** cinematic editorial introduction, institutional authority, a compelling featured acquisition, and the line 'Extraordinary lives. Available now.' Distinctive family-office / antiquarian-auction-house design.
2. **Browse the collection:** portrait-led listings of remarkable individuals, dynasties, lineages, academic and military careers, social networks, cultural legacies and discontinued houses. Curation over volume. Every listing must feel as singular and researched as an exceptional property.
3. **Inspect a dossier:** the equivalent of an estate prospectus: large portrait, ancestral homes and archival imagery, life chronology, family tree, achievements, relationships, institutional connections, distinctions, provenance chain, encumbrances, acquisition status, and price. Make it feel like buying *an entire existence*, not an itemized list of badges.
4. **Request Instantiation:** the primary action on a dossier, replacing a conventional 'Add to Cart' or 'Schedule a Viewing.' The acquisition transitions into a preview of the client's newly inherited/assembled public identity. This is fictional transformation, not an actual credential.
5. **Discover Your Valuation:** a discreet but disturbing navigation entry inviting the visitor to assess the market value of their *own* identity. This reverses the gaze: the browser discovers that they, too, can be inventory. Use a local, theatrical, invented valuation rather than collecting personal data.
6. **Private Collections:** high-end curated whole-life and whole-dynasty offerings; the most prestigious acquisitions are entire houses, not individuals.

### Example fictional listings — illustrative copy, not historical claims
- **Admiral Edmund Vale**, 1812–1889, Hampshire — Military provenance / Estate Release. Three naval campaigns, two royal commendations, tradition of service. Indicative £2,850,000.
- **The Ashcombe Line**, established 1638, Oxfordshire — Dynastic provenance / Continuity. Eleven generations of academic distinction, parliamentary association, selective philanthropy. Indicative £18,400,000.
- **Professor Julian Wren**, 1924–1997, Cambridge — Academic provenance / Nullified. A celebrated intellectual legacy separated from its original custodians. Indicative £740,000.
These are fictional sample subjects; build original distinct portraits and supporting imagery for each. Do not imply these people or their claimed honors are real.

### Dossier commercial details
Each acquisition should include a formal inventory with fields such as: Name (Available for Reassignment), Ancestral Continuity (Eleven Generations), Institutional Associations (Four), Original Custodians (Displaced), Reputational Encumbrances (Resolved), Acquisition Status (Immediate Possession). The euphemistic bureaucratic precision is the joke. Keep the emotional weight of a life and its history; do not reduce it to a simplistic game-stat sheet.

### Interaction priorities
The core interaction is **browsing → becoming fascinated with a life → opening its dossier → requesting instantiation → witnessing its provenance attached to the visitor**. Secondary actions include browsing acquisition channels (Estate Release, Nullification, Continuity), private collection requests, and the self-valuation reveal. The 'DECLARE ORIGINAL HOLDER NULL' action can appear contextually within a Nullification dossier; it should not dominate the site or imply that the user can nullify real people's rights. Use fully fictional characters and locally generated fictional paperwork.

### Creative and implementation quality bar
Every portrait, building, document and detail must belong to its subject. Avoid reused hero images, repeated photos, random mismatched imagery, generic cards, and cramped mobile gutters. Editorial hierarchy, archival authenticity, restrained motion, a richly detailed individual dossier and credible absurd pricing are essential. The site should remain entirely in character; explanations of satire belong in this README, not on the public page. The institution must seem to believe its own doctrine completely.

## Proposed division — Rehabilitation & Repatriation (2026-11-04)
**Concept captured; not committed to the initial build.** HERITAGE's Distressed Provenance division treats historical notoriety as an illiquid identity asset with extraordinary reputational encumbrances. The signature conceit: **the worse the reputation, the higher the rehabilitation fee**. A prospective client is not necessarily buying the identity; they may be purchasing the institution's impossible promise to make it socially desirable again.

### The Unrecoverable Collection
A restricted, austere division for names whose historical liabilities exceed conventional stewardship. Adolf Hitler is an extreme thought experiment: overwhelming name recognition combined with responsibility for Nazi aggression, genocide and the Holocaust. The institution's proposed separation of reputational value from historical responsibility is morally grotesque; the satire depends on stating the atrocities accurately, never denying, excusing or minimizing them. **Do not render Hitler or other perpetrators as glamorous, collectible luxury merchandise.** If used at all, real perpetrators appear only as historically accurate, non-purchasable case studies, not interactive acquisitions. Invented composite disgraced figures may serve as interactive dossiers.

### Institutional voice / sample
- Division: **REHABILITATION & REPATRIATION**
- Collection: **THE UNRECOVERABLE**
- Descriptor: 'For identities whose historical liabilities exceed conventional stewardship capabilities.'
- Dossier fields: Reputational Encumbrances — Exceptional; Public Recognition — Near Universal; Acquisition Terms — By Private Negotiation.
- The institution's chilling fictional doctrine: 'Historical responsibility remains with the original holder. Reputational value may be reassigned independently.'
- No triumphant military aesthetics, heroic portraits, celebratory framing, or exculpatory revisionism. Use heavily annotated, austere, documentary styling; explicit historical record remains intact.

### Editorial role
This is a boundary-testing satirical idea, not necessarily a homepage product. It can be omitted entirely, held as an internal concept, or represented by invented historical villains in a later release. Do not let the most notorious case overwhelm the core property-like curation of exceptional lives.
