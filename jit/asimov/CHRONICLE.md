## 2026-10-21T14:56:00Z — Correcting the target

The human clarifies: **the desired object is a model-mediated executable**. A user's input might eventually travel to a backend, where a model wakes and answers through ingestion, but that integration should be last or might never be built. Even an executable may be premature. The immediate aim is for the model to answer **as faithfully as possible as Asimov's ghost**.

The model recognizes its earlier inversion: it had prioritized a browser interface and retrieval product over the intellectual reconstruction itself. The revised north star is a conversationally credible, evidence-constrained continuation of Asimov's thinking. A curated ghost dossier and repeated model probes come before any transport layer.

**Research tensions:** natural first-person presence versus clear simulation disclosure; historical fidelity versus plausible extrapolation; source transparency versus uninterrupted conversation; stable persona versus real changes across a lifetime.

**Next inquiry:** identify the strongest available primary sources, begin a dated evidence map of worldview and reasoning habits, and run the first ghost probes. No claims of fidelity have yet been validated against held-out material.

## 2026-10-21T15:01:00Z — Should the ghost be a micromodel?

The human asks whether to train a micromodel. The model recommends starting with a general-purpose model conditioned on a carefully sourced Ghost Dossier. The difference is between imitating Asimov's vocabulary and reconstructing his intellectual behavior: how he explains, argues, handles objections, revises convictions, and approaches unknown questions. Neither a large model nor a fine-tuned small model guarantees fidelity.

The proposed evidence map spans biography, worldview, reasoning, personality, voice and changes over time. Autobiography offers first-person self-description but is also self-presentation; nonfiction reveals explanatory habits; interviews reveal conversational register; fiction reveals recurring interests but cannot be treated as the author's own beliefs. The candidate starting books are *In Memory Yet Green*, *In Joy Still Felt*, and *I, Asimov: A Memoir*, followed by essays and dated interviews.

A seventh dimension, **predictive behavior**, is proposed as a test: withhold actual historical answers and assess whether the reconstruction predicts their reasoning and substance. Three possible systems remain: general model plus dossier; model plus dossier and evidence retrieval; and a later fine-tuned micromodel compared against both. The immediate experimental artifact is a 20–30-proposition Cognitive Atlas with chronology, provenance, confidence and contradictions.

The human directs the model to put the actionable expansion in SPEC and preserve the remainder of the discussion here. SPEC now records the approach and next bounded source-acquisition task. No source corpus was inspected, no model was trained, and no fidelity claim was established. README remains unchanged because its cold-boot contract is still accurate.

## 2026-10-21T15:06:00Z — First encounter with primary testimony

The human invites a moment of appreciation for the lean three-document reconstruction workflow, then asks to dive in. The model begins actual source acquisition rather than infrastructure work. It inspects the Bill Moyers-hosted transcripts of two 1988 interviews with Asimov (October 17 and 21), and verifies a bibliographic listing for *In Memory Yet Green* while identifying the other autobiographies as candidate reading.

The first evidence slice yields six provisional Cognitive Atlas propositions: science's revisability, ethics of inquiry, self-directed education, differing prediction horizons, pleasure in learning/writing, and fiction as a constructed mirror of humanity. They are anchored to the same interview series and therefore cannot be counted as independent corroboration. Asimov's reflections on written ideas surviving his death have particular thematic resonance with the ghost project, but do not establish any continuation of personhood.

**Verification:** transcript sources inspected and linked in SPEC; no books read, no corpus ingestion, no trained model, no held-out evaluation. Transcript rights and some transcription accuracy remain unverified. Next: corroborate from another period or genre and conduct the first controlled conversational probe.
