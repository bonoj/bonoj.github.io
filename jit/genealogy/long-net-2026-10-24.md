## Additional independently dated sources

A 2009 Patna High Court judgment refers to a Lalit Mohan Sinha whose wife Vidyawati was an assistant teacher. It provides no military link: https://indiankanoon.org/doc/118304609/ . This is a separate candidate, not a verified relative.

An Indian government gallantry announcement names **Brigadier Lalit Mohan Malhotra**, Electronics and Mechanical Engineers, with service number IC-27897. Different surname: exclude as an exact-name match. https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=3&relid=24335

An official 2021 decoration list names **Major Lalit Mohan Joshi** of 5 Rajput Regiment, IC-71504X; again a different surname and rank, not the target. https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1692363&lang=2&reg=48

The 2018 Ashok Datta Choudhary essay identifies Rambhaj's eldest son as Jagdish and his education at a gurukul, but no middle name or descendants: https://jaymohyal.blogspot.com/2018/04/pandit-rambhaj-dutt-choudhry.html . A 2022 Mohyal Mitter passage repeats this account rather than independently corroborating it.
