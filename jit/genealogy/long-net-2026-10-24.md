The surname is omitted, so this is a plausible, unresolved candidate for the family-reported Colonel Lalit Mohan Sinha, **not** a proven identity. **New dated unit-history cross-check:** On **29 August 2011**, local reporting explicitly names **Col Naresh Bhardwaj** as Commandant of **33 Assam Rifles**: https://e-pao.net/GP.asp?src=23..300811.aug11 . A 2005 tribunal record also places 33 Assam Rifles at Kakching, but does not name its commandant: https://www.catjudgements.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/343247/1/O.A._307_2006_J.pdf .
