# RESOLVA — For the treatment of unresolved circumstances

**Status:** CANONICAL CAMPAIGN + EXPERIENCE SPEC; no implemented site, real drug, or clinical study exists.

> **RESOLVA™**
> *For the treatment of unresolved circumstances.*
>
> **The circumstances may persist. Your concern needn't.**

## Thesis

RESOLVA is a fictional pharmaceutical brand whose product is clinically effective at reducing a patient's insistence that something is wrong **without correcting the underlying problem**. It does not need to be a placebo or a simple scam. The company has engineered a treatment, a classification system, a trial endpoint, and a reimbursement model in which silencing persistent complaints counts as therapeutic success.

The institution never winks. It genuinely believes that persistent recognition of an unresolved problem is itself the treatable pathology.

**Core inversion:** Medicine exists to relieve suffering and restore health. RESOLVA defines the patient's demand for meaningful change as the disease, and the disappearance of that demand as recovery.

## Distinguish the layers

1. **The underlying circumstance:** an unresolved material, social, institutional, or medical problem.
2. **The patient's recognition:** continued awareness that the circumstance remains unresolved.
3. **The invented diagnosis:** Persistent Unresolved Awareness (PUA), the canonical public-facing fictional condition; PRSD may survive only as a deprecated internal research term, a fictional classification for continued insistence on a solution.
4. **The treatment:** RESOLVA, which suppresses the patient's inclination to revisit, question, escalate, or seek correction.
5. **The measured outcome:** Reduction in Unnecessary Persistence (RUP), an invented endpoint tracking diminished complaint or follow-up behavior.
6. **The institutional victory:** clinicians report fewer repeat visits; insurers report fewer escalations; employers report improved acceptance; the patient stops asking.

The sinister mechanism is not that the drug fails its chosen endpoint. **It succeeds. The endpoint is wrong.**

## Brand and visual direction

The supplied RESOLVA hero campaign image is the **binding visual reference**. It depicts the canonical woman and campaign branding. See the detailed canonical visual and experience specification below. Preserve its human warmth, photographic naturalism, giant translucent typography, warm stone/cream palette, deep desaturated teal lettering, restrained cyan accent, and deliberate contradiction between serenity and unresolved reality.

No haunted hospital, black comedy imagery, villainous CEO, obvious parody badge, or jokey drug commercial. The institutional confidence is the horror. Language should be restrained, precise, compassionate, and quietly circular.

The brand's claim should become more disturbing the longer it is examined, without suddenly changing tone.

## Proposed experience

A responsive fictional pharmaceutical website, with an intentional progression:

1. **Patient landing:** a reassuring campaign for people exhausted by unresolved circumstances. Present the fictional condition as a familiar, exhausting pattern.
2. **Condition education:** a compassionate symptom inventory: repeatedly requesting clarification, returning to unresolved questions, seeking second opinions, documenting discrepancies, or expecting follow-through.
3. **Clinical evidence:** interactive invented trial charts showing large improvements in RUP and dramatic reductions in follow-up burden. Reveal exactly what the primary endpoint measures.
4. **Physician portal:** treatment algorithm and a beautifully typeset clinical-study summary. The doctor can classify persistent questioning as an adverse burden and patient silence as response.
5. **Patient journey:** follow one fictional person whose original problem never changes while their willingness to challenge it steadily disappears.
6. **Outcomes dashboard:** show the institutional beneficiaries of treatment—patient encounters avoided, claims closed, appeals abandoned, workplace disruptions reduced.
7. **Prescribing information:** a plausible-looking but explicitly fictional internal dossier. The most revealing detail may be an adverse-event table in which 'continued concern' is counted as treatment resistance.

The public-facing experience stays in character, but the implementation must not masquerade as an actual approved drug or solicit real medical decisions. Preserve the sincere in-world campaign voice. The experience must still be distinguishable from actual medical guidance through clear contextual identification of the work as fiction in an accessible information surface, without making the hero a jokey disclaimer.

## Signature interaction — the unresolved case

A visitor opens a **fictional** patient case. The underlying circumstance is displayed as an unchanging factual record. A second panel shows the patient's response over successive treatment visits:

- **Baseline:** "This still hasn't been fixed."
- **Week 2:** "I'm trying not to dwell on it."
- **Week 6:** "I don't think following up would help."
- **Week 12:** "I am satisfied with my progress."

The unchanged underlying circumstance remains visible throughout. The clinical chart proudly reports a statistically impressive reduction in RUP. A clinician signs off: **TREATMENT GOAL ACHIEVED.**

The interaction should not rely on a cheap jump scare or a last-second reveal. The visitor notices that the treatment has improved the metric while leaving reality untouched.

## Sample invented clinical study

**Study:** RESOLVE-3, randomized, fictional, 12-week controlled trial.

**Primary endpoint:** RUP — reduction in patient-initiated attempts to reopen an unresolved matter.

**Illustrative results (invented, not medical data):**
- 81% reduction in repeat inquiries in the RESOLVA arm.
- 67% reduction in requests for independent review.
- 92% clinician-reported improvement in consultation efficiency.
- 0% demonstrated change in the underlying circumstances (not a prespecified endpoint).

These are placeholders for narrative design, not evidence, claims of efficacy, or a template for actual pharmaceutical promotion. The reference campaign artwork includes the fictional label “(resolvate) 50 mg tablets”; preserve that as **visual canon only**, not as a prescribing recommendation. Do not generate actual administration, dose schedules, chemical formulations, or medical instructions.

**Interpretive hook:** A trial can be internally rigorous and still measure the wrong thing. The horror is not fabricated statistics alone; it is the carefully engineered definition of success.

## Institutional extensions

**Payer coverage:** Reimbursement is approved when a patient demonstrates costly persistence. The more legitimate the unresolved problem, the stronger the commercial incentive to suppress follow-up.

**Provider quality:** Patient satisfaction scores rise when patients no longer articulate dissatisfaction. A hospital celebrates falling complaints while the underlying incident rate remains constant.

**Workplace benefit:** Corporate purchasers offer RESOLVA as an employee-wellness benefit for workers experiencing unresolved workplace circumstances. The employer reports improved resilience.

**Postmarketing surveillance:** Continued requests for clarification are coded as recurrence. Voluntary disengagement is coded as remission. No one needs to falsify a record; the taxonomy does the work.

These are optional second-layer experiences. Do not build a sprawling portal before proving the central patient-case interaction.

One excellent mobile-first landing page, one fictional case, one interactive 12-week timeline, and one clinical endpoint chart. A secondary physician-study view can explain the metric. No backend, account creation, payments, actual prescription workflow, or user health-data capture.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Visitor initially understands why the treatment sounds appealing.
- The patient case is emotionally credible and specific, not an abstract KPI joke.
- The original circumstance remains unchanged and visible.
- Every treatment milestone can be explored by touch and keyboard.
- The chart's measured endpoint is legible and internally consistent.
- The institution remains serenely convinced it has helped.
- A visitor can distinguish the work from a real medical product before relying on it.
- No real drug claims, medical recommendations, or personal health-data collection.

## Creative discipline

- **Do not merely make a drug that does nothing.** Make a system that achieves a measurable but perverse outcome.
- **Do not simply announce that pharma is evil.** Let credible forms, charts, workflows, and incentives establish the accusation.
- **Do not confuse sedation with resolution.** The target is *the impulse to seek correction*, not necessarily consciousness or feeling.
- **Do not flatten the patient into a statistic.** The visitor must understand what remains wrong.
- **Do not make the fictional study pass as actual evidence.** Invented data is storytelling material.
- **Do not import TENDER, HUMANE, HERITAGE, or PATERNAL mechanics.** This is its own pharmaceutical institution.

## Open decisions

- Should the central unresolved circumstance be medical, bureaucratic, familial, employment-related, or a composite?
- Does RESOLVA claim to be a daily medication, an administered intervention, or a broader branded care program? Avoid specifying real pharmacology until the fiction's form is chosen.
- Is the physician portal accessible from the outset or discovered after the patient journey?
- Should the strongest ending be the patient's own testimony, the clinical chart, or the payer's reimbursement approval?
