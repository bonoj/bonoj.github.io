# CDC — 198-center fictional appointment allocation matrix

**Status:** deterministic first-pass editorial distribution, not approved geographic epidemiology. Source: Sigils People index (198 country/territory codes, 20 portraits each). Every center receives one title from each of the three approved catalogs. This is a complete center-by-center mapping, not a real-world prevalence model. It deliberately does **not** infer infections, vector habitat, health risk, or pathogen distribution from country of origin. Center codes are source country/territory identifiers; no addresses or physical laboratory locations are implied.

All 103 titles remain approved and available in [VIRAL.md](VIRAL.md), [BACTERIAL.md](BACTERIAL.md), and [PARASITIC.md](PARASITIC.md). This matrix is a **baseline fictional staffing allocation**; additional appointments may be assigned to individual facilities. Each center has 20 facilities, each facility may hold multiple appointments, and BSL grade is per appointment. Regional ecological customization requires separate editorial review and must not be mistaken for actual epidemiological evidence.

**Allocation rule:** sorted source country codes, three independently rotated approved catalogs; deterministic and reproducible. This is designed for even fictional coverage rather than a claim of country-specific disease prevalence. A later editorial pass can give centers distinct institutional identities while preserving the approved title vocabulary.
