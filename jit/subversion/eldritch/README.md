# ELDRITCH LAW — one continuous practice

**Status:** Concept and experience specification; no implementation approved yet.
**Home:** `jit/subversion/eldritch/`
**Lineage:** Subversion institutional satire; adjacent to [INVARIANT](../invariant/README.md) and its eighteen-person roster, itself inherited from Golden Standard. Independent experience, not an INVARIANT reskin.

## Premise

**ELDRITCH LAW** is an established, prestigious legal practice occupying a warm, expensive ancestral estate reminiscent of the Roivas mansion in *Eternal Darkness*: polished timber, portraits, archives, inherited grandeur, domestic familiarity, and slowly impossible spatial relationships.

Its eighteen apparently separate partners are **eighteen three-dimensional cross-sections of a single four-dimensional being**. They are not costumes, avatars, remotely piloted humans, or a hive mind. Each section can appear independent, interact with other sections, and hold a coherent human identity, while remaining continuous with the same higher-dimensional organism. The geometry is the premise, not a demand for a physically rigorous 4D renderer.

The firm is not secretly *breaking* the law. It knows the larger law. Legal practice is the being's way of establishing, extending, preserving, and reconciling relationships with the world: a kind of anatomy or metabolism. Contracts, trusts, estates, precedent, standing, succession, and jurisdiction are not merely jokes; they are the connective tissue of the organism.

**Central line:** “Centuries of experience. One continuous practice.”

**Alternate lines:** “Your affairs have always been in order.” / “Some obligations survive death. Others precede existence.” / “One firm. Eighteen partners. An indivisible interest.”

## The satire

Conventional legal marketing already says “combined years of experience,” “one body of expertise,” “institutional memory,” “seamless service,” “living tradition,” “roots run deep,” and “continuity of counsel.” Here each cliché becomes literally, anatomically true. The audience can initially read *Eldritch* as a venerable surname, or mistake it for *elder law*. People familiar with cosmic horror recognize the double meaning sooner. Do not explain the joke on the public page.

**Important distinction from INVARIANT:** INVARIANT's eighteen human faces personify backend components and collapse into a single software intake endpoint. ELDRITCH's eighteen faces are actual visible intersections of a higher-dimensional organism. INVARIANT is a satire of machine-mediated institutional access; ELDRITCH is a satire of corporate personhood, legal continuity, inherited obligation, and institutions that precede and outlive their constituents. Preserve their independent identities and do not accidentally reveal INVARIANT's mechanism as ELDRITCH's.

### Portrait art direction and asset reuse

- **Primary source:** reuse the exact eighteen INVARIANT portrait images, maintaining the name-to-image mapping in its published implementation. Preserve source filenames or an explicit manifest mapping; never assign portraits by shuffled array order.
- **First layer — contemporary:** current portraits appear as conventional, distinguished staff photography in a refined law-firm directory. A coherent but not mechanically identical editorial treatment; real-looking skin, eyes, hair, clothing, and backgrounds. Avoid monstrous facial distortions, glowing eyes, uncanny face morphs, or supernatural symbols.
- **Second layer — archival:** selected portraits reappear in the estate as painted, engraved, daguerreotype, or early photographic records from different periods. They must remain unmistakably the **same individual** as their modern portrait, not a lookalike. Preserve distinctive facial structure; vary period clothing, pose, setting, and image medium. Do not create all eighteen archival variants by default: introduce only as many as the narrative earns.
- **Third layer — impossible continuity:** use documentary evidence, portrait dates, room placement, and repetition to establish the impossible timeline. The viewer should notice the anomaly, not have it announced in a caption. Two portraits may represent separate visible sections of the one organism, never a simple before-and-after transformation.
- **Visual system:** dark walnut and oxblood upholstery, aged gold frames, oil-paint patina, restrained natural light, subtle varnish cracks, credible archival print grain. The modern directory stays photorealistic; historical portraits adopt period-appropriate media. No blanket sepia filter, AI-smoothed faces, or cloned compositions.
- **Composition:** portrait subjects framed from chest or waist upward with eyes and faces legible on a narrow phone. Favor consistent aspect ratio (3:4 or 4:5), intentional focal point, and no baked-in names or titles. Captions and brass labels remain responsive HTML.
- **Continuity check:** compare each reused modern face with its archival variations at face-level, not just aesthetic similarity. Keep a source/variant mapping per identity and reject mismatches.
- **Site rhythm:** avoid an undifferentiated eighteen-card wall on mobile. Use a curated handful in the first view, with accessible paths to all eighteen and meaningful discoveries in the portrait gallery, practice rooms, and archives.
- **Revelation:** the eighteen are not eighteen sequential incarnations of one person. They coexist as simultaneous cross-sections of a four-dimensional being. Their repeated appearance across eras expresses persistence of the organism, not reincarnation.
- **Implementation:** existing INVARIANT assets are reused by reference or copied with provenance when building; no new images, files, or website implementation are authorized by this spec alone.

The website should never directly label its staff as “cross-sections.” Let the visitor discover continuity through portraits, contradictory dates, archived proceedings, identical testimony, and shared knowledge.

## Practice areas and legal-cosmic collisions

- **Estate planning, trusts, perpetuities:** beneficiaries and obligations older than the universe; a trust whose termination is impossible.
- **Probate and survivorship:** the deceased challenges distribution; death certificates are provisional; standing survives death.
- **Guardianship and elder / ancient law:** care and representation for beings of indeterminate age or non-linear capacity.
- **Contracts and covenants:** signatures authenticated on agreements predating the signer; obligations inherited before birth.
- **Property and easements:** an ancient right-of-way crosses a basement every three centuries; an estate extends beyond observable boundaries.
- **Jurisdiction and venue:** a court rules a defendant has always been present; venue outside ordinary spacetime.
- **Family and succession:** ancestors and descendants overlap in the same body; succession may not entail replacement.
- **Discovery and evidence:** records of future events; discovery as both civil procedure and unbearable revelation.
- **Admiralty and salvage:** claims over vessels recovered from vanished oceans.
- **Intellectual property:** rights to thoughts not yet conceived.
- **Environmental law:** protection of habitats older than the planet.

Keep the firm's language technically precise, courteous, matter-of-fact, and wholly sincere. Alternate absurd administrative precision, subtle uncanniness, and occasional truly cosmic implications. Avoid tentacle jokes, jump scares, generic occult iconography, and punchline density.

## Experience and art direction

**First impression:** a credible, elite estate-planning and private-client law firm. Expensive photography, serious typography, polished brass, dark wood, aged paper, warm lamps, windows admitting natural light, quietly occupied rooms. Roivas-estate *feeling*, not a copied game map or exact reproduction. No haunted-house splash screen, theatrical supernatural effects, or obvious fantasy branding.

**Narrative progression:**

1. **Arrival:** an immaculate law-firm homepage featuring the ancestral headquarters, practice, counsel, history, and an apparently conventional consultation path.
2. **Partners:** eighteen portraits and believable biographies; dates and continuity begin to trouble the chronology.
3. **The estate:** explore rooms or documentary sections—reception, library, portrait gallery, records office, probate chambers, archive. Rooms may have impossible adjacency or capacity, but navigation must remain legible and excellent on mobile.
4. **Case files:** discover routine-looking probate, easement, guardianship, and trust proceedings whose implications steadily widen.
5. **Discovery:** a fictional client file suggests the visitor is not necessarily the heir; the visitor may be part of the estate being inherited.
6. **Revelation without exposition:** the partners, building, archive, and legal relationships may all be intersections of one continuous higher-dimensional structure. The experience need not explicitly diagram or announce this.

The estate might itself be another cross-section of the being. Rooms larger than the building, archives outside ordinary spatial bounds, and shared memories are anatomical properties rather than magic tricks.

**Core interactive idea:** a *Discovery* case-file experience. In law, discovery obtains evidence; in cosmic horror, discovery reveals an intolerable truth. Documents and environmental evidence progressively recontextualize the firm. The visitor should have agency, not merely scroll through lore.

**Responsive principle:** mobile and desktop are one coherent experience. An explorable mansion is optional; do not force a clumsy 3D dollhouse, tiny click targets, or gratuitous interface controls. A strong editorial website with layered exploration may be better than a weak simulated mansion.

## Possible case: The Estate of the Visitor

A visitor requests ordinary estate planning. A prior trust is identified, predating the visitor and perhaps the universe. Accumulated interest is denominated in years of human life. Records reveal the visitor is not the beneficiary of an estate but an asset or interest within it. The firm remains delighted to report that all matters are in order.

This is **a story premise**, not an instruction to fabricate real debts, assert ownership of visitors, solicit sensitive data, or present legally operative documents.

## Design invariants and boundaries

- Follow [Subversion design invariants](../README.md): generous margins, intentional image geometry, unified responsive experience, no decorative arrows, no unsolicited satire labels, purposeful interactions, and visual validation.
- Preserve the approved INVARIANT identities; any reinterpretation of roles or historical portraits must be deliberate and documented.
- No real legal representation, advice, attorney-client relationship, sensitive personal-data intake, false bar credentials, fabricated real case results, or binding legal claims.
- No implementation, asset generation, or deployment is implied by this specification. Build only when requested.
