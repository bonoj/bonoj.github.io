06 / Common questions

Answers, with care.

What is a Continuant?

A residentially supported leadership successor developed to ensure familiar executive presence when an organization needs it most.

Are Continuants employees?

Not during the residential phase. Employment classification is addressed as part of placement.

What happens to the incumbent?

Our transitions are designed to minimize unnecessary disruption. Specific arrangements are handled by the sponsoring organization.

Can a Continuant decline an allocation?

Personal preferences are documented and reviewed for alignment.

How do you measure satisfaction?

Every Continuant is given the opportunity to participate in our resident experience program. Nonparticipation is recorded as a preference.

Why are there eighteen?

Everstead maintains a deliberately limited current batch to support attentive, individualized cultivation.

May I visit?

Tours are offered by appointment in designated public areas. Placement suites are not included.
