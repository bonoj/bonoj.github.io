## The proposition

REMOTE™ is a fictional premium professional-representation company. A client retains a dedicated flesh-and-blood surrogate who becomes their persistent human face inside an employer or professional organization. This is not an occasional meeting stand-in. The surrogate handles the complete visible career: interviewing, onboarding, daily standups, workplace relationships, presentations, reviews, promotions, and ordinary social presence. The client and/or their models supply the underlying expertise, decisions, deliverables, context, and real-time language. The surrogate supplies the face, voice, and apparently continuous human identity.

The central inversion: an AI video avatar rendered using an actual person. The technology is intentionally low-tech and mundane: ordinary video calls, an earpiece or teleprompter, live model-generated dialogue, contextual notes, and trained human performance. **No implants, robotics, neural control, synthetic biology, supernatural elements, or futuristic containment technology.**

REMOTE sells an entire believable professional existence, not merely attendance. It is a premium service with severe ethical and potentially legal implications: deception about who is being interviewed/employed, employment-contract breaches, identity misrepresentation, and potentially fraud depending on circumstances. This is a fictional institutional satire, not an operational service blueprint.

## The immaculate eighteen

Use the established eighteen portrait identities from the SUBVERSION portrait lineage as a recurring visual cast, with explicit source and rights verification before publication. In REMOTE they are **surrogates**, not EIGHTEEN's temporal fatality specialists and not EVERSTEAD's Continuants. They are trained human performers. Each maintains a consistent appearance, interpersonal style, and recognizable professional persona across an assigned client's career. Their apparent memory is supplied by client/model context; they do not independently produce the client's technical work or judgment.

The unsettling trait is trained **script fidelity**: no spontaneous opinions, no unsanctioned improvisation, impeccable delivery of generated material. Their value to REMOTE is their ability to seem fully present while withholding their own professional agency.

## Public institution and voice

An extraordinarily polished, credible, expensive remote-work and executive-representation brand. It should be as visually crisp, crude, and economical as GOLDEN STANDARD, not an elaborate sci-fi mythology. Premium recruitment photography, excellent spacing, restrained typography, simple service architecture, no ominous effects on the front door. The company speaks with complete sincerity.

## The facility: behind the representation

The visual counterpoint is an unglamorous, darker-than-expected physical call center. Eighteen glass-fronted, sound-controlled workrooms line a long industrial corridor, recalling the institutional display geometry of the glass cages in *The Cabin in the Woods* without borrowing its creatures or supernatural machinery.

Each room is a convincing video-call set when viewed through its webcam: warm lamp, bookshelf, plant, tasteful office backdrop. From the corridor it is a monitored workstation inside a bleak industrial operation: fluorescent lighting, cable trays, HVAC, shift schedules, supervisors, janitorial carts. The surrogates perform separate professional lives in parallel, sometimes laughing, consoling, celebrating, or arguing on cue with no genuine participation in the work being discussed. After a session, the performance stops.

No futuristic glass pods, medical horror, restraints, gore, or exotic equipment. The dread comes from ordinary labor management, commodified identity, and the disparity between the camera frame and the physical room.

## Product and narrative structure (deferred proposal)

1. **Front door:** premium enterprise workforce representation, concise promise, compelling photography, clear client-facing offerings.
2. **The eighteen:** consistent portraits, curated professional styles, continuity as a premium feature. Do not imply they are the actual employees of named real companies.
3. **Career coverage:** interview, onboarding, daily collaboration, presentation, relationship maintenance, reviews and promotion. Show scope without turning the site into practical instructions for deceiving employers.
4. **The facility:** an optional, understated glimpse into the glass-fronted operations floor, potentially through an 'Infrastructure' or 'Our Operations' section. Avoid jump scares or overt horror exposition.
5. **Quality assurance:** sterile corporate metrics such as script fidelity, presentation naturalness, unauthorized speech events, continuity, and utilization. Treat illustrative numbers as fictional, not measured outcomes.
6. **Premium human access:** an absurd paid 'Request a Human' tier that still routes to a model-scripted supervisor. A potential sharp punchline, not required functionality.

No live surrogate marketplace, real application submission, identity-impersonation workflow, operational deception playbook, actual client/employer data intake, or employer-targeting features.

## Creative principles

- **Low-tech, not science fiction.** The joke is that using real flesh is the expensive, oddly primitive alternative to a video avatar.
- **Whole-career representation, not meeting attendance.** The surrogate is the persistent public-facing professional.
- **Premium and borderline illicit.** The institution treats serious ethical and legal ambiguity as an ordinary service-quality question.
- **The same eighteen.** Reuse the portrait cast for SUBVERSION continuity, while each project retains its own independent fiction.
- **Crisp and crude.** Follow GOLDEN STANDARD's economy of concept, not its exact layout or brand.
- **Horror through architecture and labor.** The visible office is a camera set; the real office is a glass-walled performance factory.
- **Straight-faced institutional voice.** No unsolicited wink, satire disclaimer, or explanation in public-facing copy. Any real publishing constraints should be assessed separately before launch.
- **SUBVERSION invariants apply.** Independent single-page experience, intentional margins and image geometry, coherent mobile/desktop, no decorative link arrows, meaningful interactions, actual rendered QA before claiming visual quality.
