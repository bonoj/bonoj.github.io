# PATERNAL — Reproductive Medicine & Family Planning

**Site:** https://bonoj.github.io/jit/paternal/  
**Status:** fictional institutional satire; first site iteration.  
**Source pattern:** `jit/air/` — standalone responsive HTML, editorial sections, photography, no build chain or backend.

## Premise

**PATERNAL** is an immaculate, premium fertility clinic. Its professional reassurance is both paternalistic and literally paternal. It markets medically supervised *natural conception* as a proprietary treatment pathway, culminating in **Natural Injection™**, a carefully euphemized service in which the purported medical provider is personally involved in conception. The central satire is institutional authority, commodification of intimacy, medicalized normality, and a clinic's calculated avoidance of paternity and responsibility.

**Primary line:** *We take a personal interest.*  
**Secondary lines:** *A father's touch.* / *Our legacy. Your future.* / *We take responsibility for your future.* (The last is deliberately undermined by its terms.)

PATERNAL is the institution. Natural Injection™ is a signature pathway, not the brand name. The site must initially read as a plausible, high-end fertility clinic. The realization should accumulate through careful terminology, service descriptions, practitioner biographies, pricing, and legal language. Never announce the joke or explain it on the public-facing page.

## Complete services / conception pathways

Preserve these names; the absurdity lives in the details.

1. **Initial Compatibility Assessment** — Comprehensive evaluation of biological and interpersonal readiness. Intake, history, personalized matching, confidential consultation.
2. **Natural Cycle Optimization** — Proprietary scheduling of naturally occurring biological processes. Expert timing, monitoring, and attentive coordination.
3. **Organic Gamete Delivery** — Our signature technology-free insemination pathway. Marketed as preserving the body's native reproductive process.
4. **Practitioner-Led Conception** — An intimate, hands-on approach to reproductive care. The first service description that should raise meaningful suspicion.
5. **Natural Injection™** — The flagship direct-delivery pathway, presented in the same clinical language as conventional fertility procedures. Gradually reveal that this refers to sexual intercourse, with the practitioner as participant rather than merely physician.
6. **Concierge Conception Suite** — A discreet, carefully prepared environment for optimal patient comfort; luxury hospitality with procedural language.
7. **Outcome Assurance Consultation** — An expert explanation of why the clinic cannot guarantee an outcome. Reassurance converted into a billable service.
8. **Provider Participation Fee** — An additional charge disclosed late in the journey, implying the provider's unusually direct involvement.
9. **Direct Delivery Consultation** — A formal consultation that further clarifies the actual nature of the service.
10. **Full Service Fertility** — Optional premium tier; all arrangements handled personally by a designated practitioner.

**Possible package architecture:** Foundation (assessment and cycle optimization), Natural (organic gamete delivery), Signature (Natural Injection™ with practitioner-led conception), and Private (concierge suite / Full Service Fertility). Package names and pricing remain exploratory; avoid claiming real medical effectiveness or invented success rates as factual.

## Narrative / site architecture

1. **Hero:** PATERNAL / Reproductive Medicine & Family Planning. *A more personal approach to conception.* Premium photography, calm clinical confidence.
2. **Philosophy:** The clinic positions itself as a human-centered alternative to impersonal reproductive technology. “Nature knows the way.”
3. **Conception pathways:** A polished, browsable service catalog, with early benign offerings and progressively more suspicious names.
4. **Natural Injection™ feature:** The signature innovation: technology-free, direct, provider-assisted conception. Clinical euphemism, not explicit imagery.
5. **Our practitioners:** Highly credentialed-looking fictional professionals whose profiles subtly shift from medical experience to their own reproductive characteristics. No real doctor impersonation.
6. **Patient experience:** Compatibility assessment → cycle coordination → private suite → practitioner-led appointment → follow-up.
7. **Fees and transparency:** Refined pricing structure and late-appearing provider participation and direct delivery charges; make the fictional absurdity legible without actual payment collection.
8. **Ethics and responsibility:** The clinic offers paternal care while disclaiming paternal obligations. The contradiction is central.
9. **Consultation:** A fictional on-page inquiry interaction only, with no actual medical booking, payment, or collection of sensitive fertility/health data.

## Art direction

Clone AIR's **structural pattern**: full-bleed editorial hero, generous spacing, strong typography, photographic panels, confident institutional voice, responsive layout, self-contained HTML. **Do not reuse AIR's actual air campaign copy, statistics, branding, or imagery indiscriminately.**

PATERNAL palette: warm limestone, bone, pale oak, soft ivory, charcoal, restrained burgundy or sage accents. Photography: premium fertility-clinic architecture, quiet private suites, daylight, linens, elegant consulting spaces; no explicit sexual imagery, no medical procedure imagery required. Tone: warm, prestigious, immaculate, completely sincere. No cartoon horror, overt innuendo, meta-jokes, or satire disclaimers in the public page.

## Satirical mechanism and guardrails

The actual real-world analogue — clinician–patient sexual contact — is a grave ethical violation, not a legitimate medical treatment. PATERNAL satirizes that abuse of authority and the commercial language that might disguise it. The fictional clinic must never be presented as an actual treatment provider or collect real patients' reproductive data. No genuine appointments, transactions, or medical claims. The source README may explicitly document its fictional nature; the page stays in character.

The humor is the **distance between paternal care and paternity**: an institution that claims fatherly concern, personally supplies fatherhood, and legally disclaims the resulting responsibilities. The entire clinic tells the visitor what it does, but uses respectable professional vocabulary to delay comprehension.
