# PATERNAL — Reproductive Medicine & Family Planning

**Site:** https://bonoj.github.io/jit/paternal/  
**Status:** fictional institutional satire; first site iteration.  
**Source pattern:** `jit/air/` — standalone responsive HTML, editorial sections, photography, no build chain or backend.

## Premise

**PATERNAL** is an immaculate, premium fertility clinic. Its professional reassurance is both paternalistic and literally paternal. It markets medically supervised *natural conception* as a proprietary treatment pathway, culminating in **Natural Injection™**, a carefully euphemized service in which the purported medical provider is personally involved in conception. The central satire is institutional authority, commodification of intimacy, medicalized normality, and a clinic's calculated avoidance of paternity and responsibility.

**Primary line:** *We take a personal interest.*  
**Secondary lines:** *A father's touch.* / *Our legacy. Your future.* / *We take responsibility for your future.* (The last is deliberately undermined by its terms.)

PATERNAL is the institution. Natural Injection™ is a signature pathway, not the brand name. The site must initially read as a plausible, high-end fertility clinic. The realization should accumulate through careful terminology, service descriptions, practitioner biographies, pricing, and legal language. Never announce the joke or explain it on the public-facing page.

## Treatment catalog — current architecture

Six actual treatments, not ten equally weighted cards:

1. **Fertility Assessment** — diagnostic testing and specialist consultation.
2. **Ovulation Induction** — supported ovulation and cycle monitoring.
3. **Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)** — conventional insemination.
4. **In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)** — laboratory-assisted fertilization.
5. **Donor Conception** — donor matching and coordination.
6. **Natural Injection™** — PATERNAL's proprietary direct-conception pathway with personal practitioner participation.

**Packages, not treatments:** Essential (traditional care), Natural (cycle optimization), Signature (Natural Injection™, matching, direct delivery), Private (Full Service Fertility and Concierge Conception Suite).

**Journey, not treatments:** Initial Compatibility Assessment → Natural Cycle Optimization / coordination → Direct Delivery Consultation → practitioner participation → follow-up.

**Fees and services ancillary to treatment:** Provider Participation Fee, Concierge Conception Suite, Outcome Assurance Consultation. These belong in fee disclosures and patient experience, not the treatment catalog.

**Reveal:** Conventional treatment catalog first; Natural Injection™ as sixth offering and full-width feature; practitioner profiles introduce biological compatibility; packages explain escalating participation; governance separates biological contribution from paternal obligation. Keep the institution entirely in character on the public site, without fictional/satirical qualification language.

## Art direction

Clone AIR's **structural pattern**: full-bleed editorial hero, generous spacing, strong typography, photographic panels, confident institutional voice, responsive layout, self-contained HTML. **Do not reuse AIR's actual air campaign copy, statistics, branding, or imagery indiscriminately.**

PATERNAL palette: warm limestone, bone, pale oak, soft ivory, charcoal, restrained burgundy or sage accents. Photography: premium fertility-clinic architecture, quiet private suites, daylight, linens, elegant consulting spaces; no explicit sexual imagery, no medical procedure imagery required. Tone: warm, prestigious, immaculate, completely sincere. No cartoon horror, overt innuendo, meta-jokes, or satire disclaimers in the public page.

## Satirical mechanism and guardrails

The actual real-world analogue — clinician–patient sexual contact — is a grave ethical violation, not a legitimate medical treatment. PATERNAL satirizes that abuse of authority and the commercial language that might disguise it. The fictional clinic must never be presented as an actual treatment provider or collect real patients' reproductive data. No genuine appointments, transactions, or medical claims. The source README may explicitly document its fictional nature; the page stays in character.

The humor is the **distance between paternal care and paternity**: an institution that claims fatherly concern, personally supplies fatherhood, and legally disclaims the resulting responsibilities. The entire clinic tells the visitor what it does, but uses respectable professional vocabulary to delay comprehension.
