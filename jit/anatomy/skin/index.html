One extracted implicit surface over the entire body: torso, head, neck, arms, legs, hands and feet. Smooth unions merge anatomical volumes before polygonization, rather than overlapping visible meshes. Arm and leg poses regenerate the skin. This tests continuity, not production-ready anatomy: topology changes as the pose changes, thin joints can bulge, and this is not a skinned rig.
