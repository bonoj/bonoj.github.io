## The game

One mobile viewport, one HTML/CSS/Canvas file. Tiffany 🧛‍♀️ survives converging enemies, casts spells, purchases automation, places towers and grows a living civilization. Five lands and their shared identities: ☀️ Plains/prism, 💧 Island/lightning, 💀 Swamp/poison, 🔥 Mountain/fire, 🌳 Forest/vines. Keep the discovery-driven, whimsical progression; do not invent tutorials, panels, or new UI without a request.

- **Economy and HUD:** five starting hearts; ordinary kills award 2 coins and every eighth kill a gem. Health is visible immediately; kills, coins, gems, wheat, wood, ice, herbs and stone appear only after first earned/delivered and remain discovered for that run. Fixed two-row HUD with reserved aligned slots, no wave indicator. The discreet top-left source SHA can be tapped for development currency; it is **not proof of the executing build**.
- **Drones and territory:** five distinct purchasable orbiting drones, one per land, radius 65, angular speed ±0.65 rad/s. Second drone doubles world area; five drones **or** five towers expands to 4× starting area, never twice. Drone engagement radius is half the visible horizontal world span: `w/(2*arenaScale)`.
- **Towers and Tiffany:** after two drones, Tiffany autocasts through land spells every 2 seconds independently of the player's selected tower-placement land. Tap arena to place or relocate one tower per land (five max); towers cast localized effects. At five towers, autocycle stops and tapping Tiffany toggles the five-icon Mission Command ring (currently stubs).
- **Armies and civilians:** five drones unlock the same shop slots for Cherubs, Auditors, Notaries, Phoenixes, Arborists. Each purchased division recruits up to eight one-hit combat units from its living matching tower, roughly every five seconds; recruits expire after 24 seconds and retain distinct attacks. Cherubs remain **combat-only**. Arborists additionally own 🛖/🐜 logging ants, Cherubs 🍯/🐝 wheat harvesters, and Auditors 🐚/🦀 mountain miners. Each depot has up to three workers using shared FSM, claims, movement, and equipped-actor rig; materials are earned on delivery.
- **Ecology:** Plains and Forest towers plant sprouts; Island water matures wheat/trees; Swamp withers plants and killed enemies leave bones that decay to mushrooms; Mountain fire burns plants/mushrooms and spawns volcanoes. Water/Forest/Fire pulses drive mountain succession. Shared crop cap 100, geological cap 12. Keep all systems in the **same battlefield**, not separate interior games.
- **Destruction/recovery:** enemy contact consumes the attacker and destroys one-hit towers, civilian depots and workers without kill currency. Towers recover after five seconds at their current coordinates; depots recover after five seconds at a valid site relative to their associated tower's current position. Living depots replenish workers every 1.5 seconds up to three. No duplicate land purchases, no overlapping building fallback.

## Proposed civilian building — Swamp alchemist (specified, not implemented)

- **Building/depot:** ⚗️ alchemical cauldron, associated with the 💀 Swamp land. This is a **world building icon, not a shop/upgrade icon**. Do not change any existing land, Tier I, or Tier II purchase icons or add a purchase control as part of this specification.
- **Creature:** 🪱 worm. **Equipped tool:** 🗡️ dagger. **Harvest target/cargo:** 🍄 mushroom.
- **Behavior:** worms leave their cauldron, independently find and claim existing mushrooms, wriggle to the target, slash with their dagger to harvest, visibly carry the mushroom home, and unload it into the material ledger. The dagger's slash is a rotation around its grip using the existing equipped-actor rig; no custom artwork required. Keep creature, dagger, mushroom and cauldron legible at mobile size.
- **Implementation direction:** extend the existing shared civilian worker FSM (idle → travel → work → carry → unload), equipment orientation, exclusive target claims, valid-target recovery, persistent depot placement, worker replacement and depot destruction/recovery. Do not create a parallel worker system or spawn overlapping depots. Mushrooms are already produced by swamp decomposition; harvest those actual world objects, not synthetic resource ticks.
- **Economy:** mushroom inventory and its later spending use require separate design decisions; do not silently equate mushrooms with existing herbs or invent processing recipes. Likewise, the unlock/purchase trigger is **not yet selected**. Preserve existing combat and controls.
- **Status:** approved icon/behavior specification only; no game-code implementation authorized yet.

## Proposed flying irrigators — butterfly rain (specified, not implemented)

- **Representative:** 🦋 butterfly, a fast flying civilian ecology worker. **Effect:** 🌧 rainclouds left behind as the butterfly travels; rainclouds are persistent world effects, not the butterfly's body or a new combat unit.
- **Targets and priority:** select eligible active 🌋 volcanoes first, then 🌱 sprouts from either Plains or Forest. Ignore active volcanoes inside the 🔥 Mountain tower's effective fire radius when choosing targets. Retarget when eligibility changes; avoid redundant targeting of patches already covered by effective rain. Fast autonomous flight and idle wandering should reuse the existing actor/worker grammar where practical.
- **Rain footprint and duration:** each deposited 🌧 hovers in place for **30 seconds**, with a useful area footprint covering a local patch of sprouts or a volcano and nearby ecology, rather than a single point. Butterfly departs to seek another target while the rain persists. Rain performs the existing Island water transformations: 🌱 matures to 🌾 Plains wheat or 🌳 Forest tree according to its origin; 🌋 cools to the next mountain stage. Apply existing ecological transition/timer rules where compatible, rather than inventing a second unrelated ecology.
- **Fire override:** while a 🌧 footprint covers an object, it **overrides 🔥 fire's ability to destroy crops/trees or change any mountain stage** within that footprint. This applies to all crop/tree stages and all mountain stages, including fire-tower pulses, scheduled fire transitions and volcanic burning. Pending fire-driven changes must not execute under rain protection; guard at application time, not only when scheduled. Once rain expires, ordinary fire behavior resumes for subsequent effects. Rain protection is spatial and lasts for the cloud's remaining lifetime.
- **Building:** this profession requires its own persistent, nonoverlapping civilian home/depot, with icon **not yet selected**. Island already has 🐚 crab miners; irrigators are an additional profession and must not replace the crab depot or its workers. Unlock/upgrade icon and purchase condition are **not yet specified**; do not invent UI.
- **Status:** design specification only; no game-code changes authorized. Preserve existing tower/combat mechanics outside the described rain/fire ecological interaction.
