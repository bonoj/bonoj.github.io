## Phase 0 — 2026-11-04
Deterministic PRNG, versioned identity, canonical fifteen-function graph, macroplan, obligations, and event-sourced state.

## Phase 1 — 2026-11-04
First fifteen-scene Thriller fixture, Fountain export, corpus tests. One fixed plot proved byte uniqueness is not semantic diversity.

## Phase 2 — 2026-11-04
Three authored causal architectures: civic-records exposure, flood rescue, emergency broadcast recovery. Nine typed narrative events per film; replayable state.

## Phase 2.1 — 2026-11-04
Per-architecture scene locations, INT/EXT headings, explicit participant movement, and spatial-state replay checks.

## Phase 2.2 — 2026-11-04
Added a **separate experimental dialogue intention planner**: two conversational tactics per scene, seeded independently, with speaker validation and deterministic fixtures. It currently proposes tactic-labelled utterances from a finite library. **The screening room continues to use the more context-specific authored dialogue**; generic tactic lines are not substituted into the published script because that would reduce narrative quality. The dialogue plans are available as diagnostics in scene ASTs. This separates the future conversation planner from the renderer without pretending the current phrases are naturalistic. Tests check deterministic plans across 100 films and 1,500 scenes.

**Next:** replace generic tactic utterances with context-aware moves derived from character goals, knowledge, and the scene transaction, then gate adoption against the authored baseline. Need a second distant genre to test universality. Feature-length, deep diversity, and full dialogue synthesis remain unimplemented.
