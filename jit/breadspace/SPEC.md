# BREADSPACE — SPEC

## Problem space (verbatim conversation capture)

**BREADSPACE: THE CAKE IS A LIE.** 🎂

I think we've stumbled into a surprisingly good premise: a world where **everything is made by shooting ingredients at other ingredients**, and the gun is the only tool humanity remembers how to operate.

But before inventing a villain or a story, we should map the chemistry of the world.

The plot might emerge from the production chains themselves.

## 3. The interesting part: everything connects

Illustrative dependencies:

🌾 → 🍞 → 🎂

🐄 → 🧀 → 🎂

🐔 → 🥚 → 🎂

The interesting constraint is **shared ingredients**.

🌾 feeds cows and chickens, but also makes bread. 🥛 makes butter, cheese, and ice cream. 🥚 makes breakfast or cake. 🔥 cooks almost everything but can also destroy it.

That means recipes aren't isolated crafting menus. They're a connected world.

## 4. The cake is a lie

🎂

THE PROMISED OBJECT

What if Breadspace starts as an utterly straightforward baking simulator?

A giant illuminated cake sits behind glass at the far end of the facility. The facility promises that everything you make will bring you closer to it.

You shoot wheat. Make bread. Feed a cow. Extract milk. Make butter. Raise chickens. Collect eggs. Assemble increasingly elaborate foods.

Every successful transformation unlocks a new physical ingredient in the world.

But the cake recipe never completes.

Not because we've hardcoded an impossible objective, but because **cake is not a primitive object**. It's a composite of materials, processes, and dependencies. The facility has confused the *picture* of a cake with the thing itself.

And perhaps, when we finally do make cake, the glass opens to reveal...

**Another cake.**

## 5. What I'd establish before writing the plot

Three different kinds of transformation:

- **Production:** 🌾 + 🪨 → 🥣. Inputs are consumed, output is created.
- **Harvesting:** 🐄 + 🌿 → 🐄 + 🥛. The producer survives.
- **Assembly:** 🍞 + 🥩 + 🧀 → 🍔. Multiple finished products combine.

A fourth emerges naturally: **destruction**. 🔥 + 🌾 → 💨, or 🔥 + 🍞 → ⚫.

This gives the gun four fundamentally different things to do, even though the player only ever taps to cycle ammunition and taps to shoot.

And here's the part I like most: **we don't actually need a scripted plot yet.**

We need a small, internally consistent emoji chemistry. Once the player can accidentally turn a chicken into dinner while trying to bake a cake, Breadspace will start writing its own story.

The cake can wait. 🎂
