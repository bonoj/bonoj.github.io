## Operating rules

- Keep JIT work isolated under `jit/`. The root `https://bonoj.github.io/` is protected; do not change its appearance, behavior, dependencies, or publisher as a side effect.
- Use established source, build, publishing, and recovery machinery; do not replace it speculatively.
- Read back writes and distinguish a commit, a Pages build, and independently verified live browser behavior.
- Preserve existing experiments. Follow per-experiment instructions before edits.
- Hermeticon is a separate project (`bonoj/Hermeticon`); use its own handoff and deployment machinery.
