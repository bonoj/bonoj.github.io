# UNIT OLOGY — CHRONICLE

## 2026-10-27 — Birth
The question began with the absence of an obvious official Church of Unitology website. Rather than a wiki about the institution, the ambition became a website the church itself would plausibly operate: a complete, sober, public-facing institutional artifact.

## Hausgeist precedent
Inspected jit/hausgeist/index.html. Its premium real-estate presentation, editorial ivory palette, restrained burgundy/brass accents, property records, and quietly impossible administrative discrepancies established the core narrative principle: the institution never acknowledges that its own records are horrifying.

## Real-world institutional reference
Discussed the public-facing Church of Scientology's organizational structure as a reference for the types of pathways an institution might offer: introductory teaching, community, services, membership and archives. This is structural research, not a plan to reproduce real-world copy or branding.

## A building made of HTML
Proposed a website with a day/night cycle whose DOM elements cast shadows as if they were columns, cornices, and architectural slabs. One shared virtual sun vector and virtual depth model create the illusion of inhabitable architecture without 3D geometry. Time of day changes the emotional tenor without changing content.

## The Continuity Wing
The church's canonical preservation of deceased adherents suggested an invented institutional wing devoted to whole-body contributions, biological continuity, organ and tissue inventories, preservation, and a reliquary. Public language should frame these as normal acts of stewardship. No actual donation collection.

## Descent and flaying
Navigation should feel simultaneously like descending into a building and stripping away its skin. Each deeper page reveals the layer beneath the previous one; reversing direction rebuilds the façade. Sanctuary, Doctrine, Membership, Continuity, Anatomy, Reliquary, and Convergence are the proposed strata. The deeper experience becomes more intimate, clinical and geometrically reduced; Convergence may return to almost featureless white.

## Seriousness invariant
User explicitly requested that the site take itself completely seriously: no fiction disclaimers, no Dead Space attribution, no winks. This applies to visitor-facing presentation. Development documents retain provenance and canon/invention distinctions, and the executable will not impersonate a real organization or solicit real-world sensitive information.

## Next action
Implement a minimal but exquisite public sanctuary, shared solar CSS lighting, and one reversible peel/descent into the Continuity Wing. Evaluate on device before expanding content or polishing.
