# COMMONWEALTH — Public launch charter (draft v0.1)

## What is being launched

A public-interest, open-source research initiative to examine how universal material security, distributed ownership and accountable machine mediation can support human autonomy. It is **not** a government, financial custodian, redistribution authority, autonomous enforcement network, or existing global coalition.

## Founding proposition

Every person should have access to a secure material foundation independent of wages, employment, inherited wealth, citizenship, productivity, or the market value of their labor. Physical feasibility, financing, delivery, rights and institutional legitimacy must be demonstrated rather than presumed.

## Three operating commitments

1. **Truthful accounting:** distinguish sourced facts, hypotheses, toy simulations, independently tested software and measured outcomes.
2. **Constitutional machine mediation:** use machines to investigate, reconcile, simulate, negotiate draft voluntary agreements and audit, never to invent legal authority or impose coercive transfers.
3. **Independent public challenge:** publish assumptions, dissent, correction history and methods; invite affected communities and independent researchers to challenge the work.

## Initial public program

- Map basic needs and physical productive capacity using licensed aggregate public datasets.
- Build reproducible, region-aware models of essential service provision, financing and broad ownership.
- Demonstrate machine-agent disagreement, independent audit, rights protection and continuity of services using synthetic fixtures.
- Develop a charter for independently selected human stewards and accessible appeals.
- Explore voluntary and lawful institutional pilots only after review, mandate and safeguards.

## Participation

Researchers: submit dataset provenance, uncertainty critiques, and reproducible model tests.
Civic organizations: identify excluded groups, service barriers and legitimate local governance arrangements.
Economists and public administrators: test cost, revenue, labor and implementation assumptions.
Engineers: reproduce tests, inspect code, propose safety and audit improvements.
Residents: describe what a genuine ability to refuse exploitation requires, without disclosing sensitive personal information.

No endorsement, partnership or institutional authority is implied by an invitation.

## No ultimatum

No threats, compulsory demands, asset seizure, automated punishments or coercive deadlines are issued by this initiative. Any future compulsory redistribution mechanism would require independently established lawful authority and enforceable due process.

## Publication gate

Before representing this as an operational initiative: verify the site, complete independent reproducibility checks, publish a dataset provenance manifest, specify a privacy/security reporting route, and make it clear who is legally responsible for the project. Do not claim independent stewards, partners or deployments until verified.

## Contact and accountability

Initial steward/maintainer: repository owner; no external organization or independent oversight board yet appointed. Until a safe public contact channel is selected, use ordinary GitHub repository collaboration channels where enabled. Do not publish private contact details.
