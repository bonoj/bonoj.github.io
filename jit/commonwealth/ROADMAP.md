## Governance milestones beyond prototype

1. Independent external review of methods and rights protections.
2. Transparent process to nominate and select human stewards under a published charter.
3. A limited, consenting civic research pilot with no consequential automated decisions.
4. Separate legal and institutional mandate before any custody or disbursement of actual resources.
5. Independent appeals, financial audits and measured service outcomes before expansion.

## Critical failure conditions

No real-world assets controlled; no lawful mandate; no independent steward body; no verified production dataset; no independently tested distributed network. None may be represented as complete without evidence.
