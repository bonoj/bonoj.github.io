# COMMONWEALTH — Universal Autonomy & Security Lab

**Status:** Initial public-interest research prototype; no redistribution implemented.  
**Canonical source:** `bonoj/bonoj.github.io` → `jit/commonwealth/` on `master`.  
**Site:** https://bonoj.github.io/jit/commonwealth/ (verify publication independently).

## Mission

Make material security and meaningful personal autonomy accessible to every existing human and every newborn, without requiring wealth, employment, citizenship, family sponsorship, or dependence on a private patron. Reduce concentrated economic power without concentrating coercive power in a new institution.

## Origin and design commitments

The initiating question is why wealth can make employment, housing, healthcare, mobility, legal representation, and personal time optional for a few while others must continuously negotiate survival. The user challenged the characterization of rental housing as sovereignty: a temporary tenancy may provide relief, but cannot alone guarantee durable control of one's circumstances. The project responds to this distinction.

A proposed global wealth ultimatum and lethal enforcement were discussed. **This project does not implement threats, coercive deadlines, targeting, autonomous sanctions, or violence.** It pursues evidence-based, lawful, publicly accountable institutional reform. No countdown or conversational turn count grants authority to harm anyone.

## Six guarantees

1. **Shelter:** safe, stable housing; meaningful protection from arbitrary displacement.
2. **Health:** essential medical, dental, mental, preventive and disability care without financial ruin.
3. **Sustenance:** adequate food, clean water, sanitation, and reliable basic energy.
4. **Economic agency:** material security sufficient to refuse exploitative work and recover from setbacks.
5. **Opportunity:** education, connectivity, accessible technology, and feasible mobility.
6. **Civil liberty:** due process, privacy, bodily autonomy, political participation and protection from arbitrary power.

These are minimum entitlements, not a claim that all outcomes or preferences can be made identical. Children must hold claims independently of parental resources.

## Three economic instruments

- **Universal essential services:** reduce the cost and insecurity of basic living.
- **Direct material security:** income supports and accessible emergency reserves.
- **Broadly distributed ownership:** durable stakes in productive assets, worker ownership, and independently protected child endowments.

Evaluate progressive taxation, inheritance reform, economic-rent capture, anti-avoidance enforcement, public asset dividends, and employee ownership as **scenarios**, not preordained answers. Test fiscal capacity, behavioral responses, investment, administrative feasibility, cross-border coordination, and unintended consequences.

## System topology

Public and permissioned evidence → documented assumptions → reproducible scenario engine → independent review → democratic institutional decisions → audited delivery → measured human outcomes.

There is **no single central wealth custodian**. Use federated jurisdictions and institutions with independent audit, contestability, transparent accounting, and separation of powers. The software advises; it does not seize assets or adjudicate guilt.

## Acceptance criteria

A user can inspect a proposed policy and determine: who benefits, who pays, which autonomy indicators change, what evidence supports the estimate, and what remains uncertain. No invented live metrics. All example values are labeled illustrative.
