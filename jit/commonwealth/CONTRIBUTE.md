# How to help COMMONWEALTH

The most useful contribution is **not money, intimidation, or a deadline**. It is choosing testable definitions and exposing weak assumptions.

## Three high-leverage decisions

1. **Minimum autonomy floor.** Describe what an adult must be able to refuse without losing shelter, food or healthcare. For example: could they stop working for six months? Is moving between cities a protected capability?
2. **Newborn claim.** Choose whether the initial model prioritizes universal services, a protected capital account, or both; and whether access should be global or jurisdictional during early pilots.
3. **Institutional failure.** Identify the worst plausible failure: corrupt custodians, exclusion of migrants, fiscal flight, inflated housing costs, loss of due process, or paternalistic restrictions on recipients.

You can reply with plain language. No forms or spreadsheets required. If you prefer, pick **one** of the above and we will encode it as a falsifiable requirement.

## Research contributions

Suggest reputable datasets and critique assumptions. Distinguish observed figures from hypotheses. Favor public aggregate information over private personal information.

## What not to do

Do not issue threats, set coercive deadlines, target people, solicit assets, or treat this prototype as authorization to seize property. The project seeks a defensible route to human security, not a mechanism for harm.

## Current working assumptions

- Six guarantees; three economic instruments.
- Federated, accountable delivery; no central omnipotent repository.
- Child rights independent of parental resources.
- Local-first simulation, not live enforcement.
- Measurable outcomes, explicit uncertainty, documented provenance.
