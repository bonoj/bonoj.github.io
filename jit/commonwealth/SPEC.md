# COMMONWEALTH — System Specification v0.1

## 0. Status and scope

Research and civic decision-support prototype, not a world government, financial institution, enforcement apparatus, or implemented public policy. Initial release is intentionally small, testable and static. All policy assumptions are illustrative unless explicitly tied to a cited dataset.

## 1. Normative objectives

**Autonomy:** the effective ability to decline coercive work, choose residence, obtain necessary care, seek remedy, speak freely, and pursue chosen ends.  
**Security:** resilient access to essential goods and rights under illness, unemployment, displacement, disability, and economic shocks.  
**Equity:** prioritized reduction of severe deprivation and inherited disadvantage, without assuming equal wealth implies equal need.  
**Universality:** guarantees apply to every existing life and to each newborn; implementation is progressive and jurisdiction-dependent.  
**Non-domination:** no private or public actor should gain unreviewable control over the necessities of another's life.

## 2. Minimum guarantees and candidate indicators

| Guarantee | Desired state | Indicators (candidate; definitions to be validated) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shelter | Secure housing | Homelessness, forced displacement, housing cost burden |
| Health | Essential care accessible | Unmet need, catastrophic spending, dental access |
| Sustenance | Basic material needs met | Food insecurity, safe water, sanitation, energy access |
| Economic agency | Survival not conditioned on employer | Poverty, emergency savings, benefit coverage |
| Opportunity | Access to learning and mobility | Education, broadband, accessibility, travel constraints |
| Civil liberty | Rights enforceable in practice | Access to counsel, due process, independent oversight |

Disaggregate by age, geography, disability and other relevant dimensions only where lawful, privacy-preserving and empirically sound. Never create individual risk or target scores.

## 3. Distribution architecture

Three coupled layers:
A. Essential services funded and delivered through accountable local/national systems.  
B. Portable income and emergency security entitlements with accessible appeals.  
C. Broad ownership via worker equity, public dividends, and child capital accounts with independent fiduciary protections.

Avoid a global central custodian. Interoperable, audited local funds can coordinate under treaties and public standards. Separate data stewardship, fiscal administration, independent audit, judicial review and democratic policymaking.

## 4. Candidate fiscal levers

Progressive income/capital taxation, inheritance taxation, taxes on land or economic rents, enforcement of existing obligations, anti-monopoly policy, worker ownership, and public returns from shared resources. For each lever assess gross revenue, avoidance, migration, valuation, capital formation, administration, cross-border effects, and distributional incidence. Reject any model that silently equates a wealth stock with spendable annual revenue.

## 5. Newborn rights

At birth: independent entitlement to essential services and a protected claim to a child capital endowment. The endowment's amount, vesting age, fiduciary oversight, anti-diversion safeguards, portability and inflation protection are open design questions. The goal is intergenerational opportunity, not a promise of a fixed globally affordable amount.

## 6. Scenario contract

Inputs: population, eligible persons, baseline resource gap per person per year, total taxable base, effective annual fiscal rate, delivery efficiency, annual public spending, child cohort and child endowment.  
Outputs: gross fiscal flow, net usable fiscal flow, annual resource need, funding gap/surplus, potential coverage ratio, child endowment requirement.  
Units must be explicit. This is a **fiscal toy model**, not a validated causal estimate of autonomy. Do not present a positive fiscal balance as proof of housing, health, liberty or security delivery.

## 7. Governance and safety

- No automated asset transfers, punishments, confiscation, blacklists, threats or violent targeting.
- Human institutions retain lawful authority; published decisions must permit challenge and appeal.
- Model assumptions, code, sources and revisions are auditable.
- Privacy by design: aggregate statistics, no individual dossiers.
- Independent review for conflicts of interest and corruption.
- No unilateral administrator may control funds, model evidence and enforcement.
- Periodic democratic review and sunset/revision procedures.

## 8. Evaluation

Three distinct evidence levels: **simulation** (calculated), **pilot** (observed under limited deployment), **outcome** (independently measured population impact). Never collapse these. Test for exclusion, capture, service bottlenecks, inflationary pressure, corruption, displacement of local institutions, and adverse effects on the least secure.

## 9. Research backlog

Find reliable cross-country baselines from World Bank, UNDP, WHO/UNICEF, ILO, OECD, UN-Habitat, and national statistical offices. Verify definitions, years, and coverage before importing. Quantify real annual service costs and funding capacity; compare competing fiscal regimes; study governance feasibility and rights protections. Record disagreement and uncertainty.

## 10. Milestones

M0 repository + documented principles + local interactive toy.  
M1 reproducible data provenance and region-specific baselines.  
M2 uncertainty bands and sensitivity tests.  
M3 policy incidence and distributional modeling.  
M4 independent external critique and community co-design.  
M5 voluntary, lawful institutional pilots and evaluation.

The timeline is evidence-driven, not tied to a threat or countdown.

## 11. Turn 3 decision — C-001: unconditional entitlement

**Decision:** Required individual economic contribution to qualify for the universal autonomy and security floor is **zero**. This is a normative requirement, not an empirical claim that global provisioning is already complete.

Every existing person and every newborn qualifies independently of wages, employment, productivity, market demand, inherited wealth, citizenship, disability, age, or capacity to work. Refusing employment, leaving a relationship, declining a contract, or changing residence must not cause loss of essential material security. No one must demonstrate usefulness to deserve life.

### Three service levels

- **I — Survival (unconditional):** adequate food, safe water, sanitation, shelter, essential healthcare, safety and enforceable rights.
- **II — Security and autonomy (unconditional):** stable private housing, comprehensive healthcare including dental, connectivity, education, legal recourse, mobility and a material ability to refuse exploitative dependence.
- **III — Abundance (expanding universal objective):** culture, recreation, creative tools, travel, personal space and discretionary resources. Scarce goods may require transparent fair allocation; scarcity never determines human worth.

### Production constraint

The entitlement floor is unconditional for individuals; provisioning remains subject to real labor, materials, energy, ecological and institutional constraints. Gross domestic product is not directly distributable cash. Fiscal capacity alone does not prove physical capacity. Test both.

The program must measure annual physical quantities of housing, care, food, water, energy and transport; replacement/maintenance requirements; local shortages; labor hours and skills; environmental impacts; and effective delivery.

### Contribution and incentives

Necessary work still must be performed. Meet staffing requirements through compensation, improved conditions, training, automation, cooperative arrangements and democratically authorized non-deprivation-based incentives. Do not condition basic needs on accepting a job. Additional contributions may earn discretionary rewards but not unchecked authority over other people's rights.

### Evaluation questions

1. What is the minimum physical consumption bundle for each guarantee, adjusted for local climate, household composition, accessibility and needs?
2. What proportion of current productive capacity is required to supply it sustainably?
3. Which deficits are fiscal, which are physical, and which are failures of governance or distribution?
4. How does the system preserve essential work participation when survival is unconditional?
5. Can a person actually refuse employment or leave an unsafe home without losing access to essentials?

### Epistemic status

The feasibility of a substantial universal floor is a research hypothesis requiring country-level physical resource accounts, service capacity, and credible fiscal modeling. Do not assert that every form of abundance can be delivered globally today without evidence.


## 12. Turn 4 — Institutional control and lawful transition architecture

### Purpose and boundary

Translate the universal entitlement axiom into an accountable model of real assets, effective control, legal obligations, and delivery capacity. A previously proposed ultimatum with lethal consequences is **not** an authorized project requirement. Commonwealth does not design coercive countdowns, threats, extrajudicial seizures, clandestine asset transfers, or autonomous enforcement. This section models lawful, reviewable institutional transitions.

### Five-layer asset and control ontology

1. **Beneficial ownership:** verified owners, ownership shares, intermediate entities, reporting date, source and uncertainty.
2. **Effective control:** voting rights, board appointment rights, vetoes, management agreements and practical governance authority.
3. **Legal custody:** custodians, registries, jurisdictions, liens, restrictions, competing claims and legally established beneficial interests.
4. **Public obligations:** enacted taxes, final judgments, regulated duties, and legally established liabilities; distinguish allegations from findings.
5. **Human dependence:** workers, residents, pension beneficiaries, patients, consumers and communities relying on continuous services.

Model relationships at institutional and aggregate level. No individual targeting lists, doxxing, personal asset dossiers or unauthorized data access. Do not assume an asset stock is liquid or that the legal owner is the only affected party.

### Transition pathways

A. **Voluntary agreements:** philanthropy, negotiated public-interest investment, employee ownership conversions, cooperative acquisition, voluntary trusts and transparent settlement agreements.  
B. **Legislated fiscal mechanisms:** progressive and inheritance taxes, lawful anti-avoidance measures, public dividend funds, and equitable public expenditure, subject to statutory limits and appeal.  
C. **Adjudicated remedies:** independently reviewed tax collection, restitution, fines or court-ordered transfers after valid legal process.  
D. **Regulated restructuring:** publicly authorized utility, housing or enterprise reforms with continuity of service, creditor and beneficiary protections, compensation where legally required, and judicial review.

There is no machine-issued ultimatum or enforcement power. Any lawful notice has a cited legal basis, named accountable authority, contestation route and realistic compliance period. Do not model physical force, threats, covert coercion, or unauthorized asset seizure.

### Multi-agent machine research protocol

Permitted machine roles:
- **Evidence agent:** public, provenance-tagged aggregate data and confidence annotations.
- **Economics agent:** cost, incidence, labor and physical capacity scenarios.
- **Rights agent:** due-process, privacy, civil-liberties and non-discrimination review.
- **Continuity agent:** model dependencies of housing, utilities, healthcare, pensions and food systems.
- **Audit agent:** reproduce calculations, flag conflicts, challenge assumptions.
- **Negotiation support agent:** prepare voluntary, nonthreatening policy options for authorized human participants.

Agents exchange signed, versioned proposals and evidence references, not operational commands to seize assets. No agent may self-assign legal authority. Require independent human authorization for external communication, contractual commitments or transactions. Disagreements remain visible rather than being silently averaged into consensus.

### Contingency planning: nonviolent and rights-preserving

- **Nonparticipation:** document constraints, publish aggregate impact scenarios, continue lawful negotiation and democratic policymaking.
- **Jurisdictional conflict:** identify applicable law, treaty gaps, legal remedies and unresolved claims; do not bypass controls.
- **Data gaps:** label unknown; use sensitivity bands; never infer private holdings as facts.
- **Institutional capture:** separation of duties, open audits, rotating independent reviewers, conflict disclosures, appeal and removal mechanisms.
- **Service disruption:** maintain operating capital and continuity obligations for essential institutions before changing financial claims.
- **Fiscal shortfall:** prioritize minimum guarantees, revise funding assumptions transparently and evaluate capacity expansion.
- **Machine error or compromise:** suspend proposed external actions, retain immutable provenance, human review, rollback and independent incident assessment.

### Initial data sources to validate

National accounts, central bank distributional wealth accounts, tax authority public reports, corporate registries, beneficial-ownership transparency datasets where lawful, financial disclosures, public pension accounts, World Bank, ILO, WHO, UNICEF, OECD and UN statistical series. Validate jurisdiction, date, completeness, licenses and definitions. Never claim comprehensive global ownership visibility.

### Acceptance tests

1. Given a hypothetical enterprise, distinguish legal ownership, control, custody and affected beneficiaries.
2. Given an alleged obligation, reject treating it as an enforceable debt without valid legal authority.
3. Given an ownership transition, account for worker, resident, patient, pension and creditor continuity.
4. Given conflicting machine-agent recommendations, require recorded disagreement and accountable human decision.
5. Given an unauthorized request for asset transfer or coercive ultimatum, reject it without executing external actions.
6. Given a proposed fiscal reform, trace its legal pathway, distributional impact, appeals and independent audit.

### Next deliverables

An aggregate institutional graph schema, synthetic test fixtures, provenance validator, rights/continuity stress tests, and a source-backed feasibility model. All remain decision support rather than real-world enforcement.

## 13. Turn 5 — Model-forward mediation, not machine sovereignty

### Decision

**Guarantee essential services first; distribute productive ownership in parallel; establish machine mediation as a permanent, accountable civic capability.** A machine system can advise, reconcile evidence, propose allocations, flag inconsistencies, negotiate draft voluntary terms, and audit outcomes, but does not acquire title to assets, sovereign authority, judicial powers, or an autonomous enforcement mandate.

### Evidence anchors (reviewed 2026-11-21)

- World Bank, *Poverty, Prosperity, and Planet Report 2026*: estimates 811 million people in extreme poverty in 2026 at the $3.00/day 2021 PPP threshold and 3.7 billion below $8.30/day. URL: https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/poverty-prosperity-and-planet . Estimates and nowcasts are not a direct measure of material production capacity.
- OECD, *Digital Government Outlook 2026*: 35 of 36 reporting OECD countries use AI in some government area; only 11 of 36 report formal algorithmic transparency standards, and 6 have open algorithm registers. URL: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/digital-government-outlook_0496b2bc-en/full-report/adopting-and-governing-ai-in-government_7ef312a9.html .
- OECD, *Modernising Access to Social Protection*: documents failures of automated welfare decision systems and emphasizes meaningful human review, explainability, contestability and accessible appeals. URL: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/modernising-access-to-social-protection_af31746d-en/full-report/component-8.html .

### Governance division

- **Humans and legitimate institutions:** establish rights, democratic constraints, legal authority, consent, appeals, enforceable commitments, and accountable stewardship.
- **Machine mediators:** estimate needs, reconcile provenance-tagged public aggregate data, simulate allocations, identify service bottlenecks, compare policies, monitor outcomes, detect conflicting claims and recommend review.
- **Independent oversight:** challenge both machine outputs and human institutional actions, publish error analyses and suspend flawed processes.

### Multi-agent workflow

1. **Needs agent** identifies regional unmet essential service needs and uncertainty.
2. **Capacity agent** models physical housing, food, health, energy and infrastructure supply.
3. **Economics agent** estimates fiscal feasibility, incidence, behavioral response and resource trade-offs.
4. **Rights agent** checks non-discrimination, privacy, due process and accessibility.
5. **Capture agent** tests concentration of institutional power and conflicts of interest.
6. **Adversarial auditor** reproduces estimates, highlights disagreement, detects unsupported claims.
7. **Human authorization gateway** determines whether any proposed external communication or transaction is permitted. Default deny.

Agents exchange structured claims with source, observation date, jurisdiction, assumptions, uncertainty, model version and reviewer status. A consensus of machines is not evidence of truth or authorization.

### Core invariants

- No individual loses essential services because of nonemployment or refusal to participate.
- No machine-issued ultimatum, coercive enforcement, seizure, threat, or punishment.
- No agent can both allocate and independently approve its own decision.
- No external action without scoped, revocable human authorization.
- All consequential decisions have accessible explanations, appeals and meaningful remedies.
- Keep aggregate research distinct from individualized high-stakes decisions.
- Evaluate exclusion and false-positive harms, including when apparently efficient algorithms deny people support.
- Model continuity of pensions, housing, healthcare, utilities, food supply and legitimate creditors in institutional reforms.

### Next implementation target

Build a **synthetic regional resource allocation lab**: three regions with different needs and physical capacities, explicit minimum guarantees, a deterministic constrained allocation algorithm, a rights checker, an adversarial auditor and a human-review queue. Show unmet needs and uncertainty rather than inventing a global success metric. This is a research simulator only.

### Unresolved research question

Which minimum entitlements can be provisioned immediately from extant regional capacity, which require infrastructure expansion, and which require international transfers? Require data before answering.

## 14. Turn 6 — Constitutional stewardship and bounded enforcement

### Decision

Commonwealth may source candidates, facilitate transparent elections and sortition, monitor compliance, and support legally authorized redistribution, but **may not elect its own overseers, manufacture jurisdiction, seize assets, or command coercive operations**. The legitimacy chain must originate outside the machine system: participating people and jurisdictions → enacted charter and lawful mandates → elected/selected accountable stewards → narrowly delegated machine tasks → independent review and public appeal.

### Stewardship constitution

- **Open candidacy:** transparent eligibility criteria, accessible nominations, conflict declarations, independent identity/credential verification where legally appropriate, privacy protections and appeal.
- **Representative chamber:** affected residents elect representatives through locally lawful, independently administered voting; machine translation and public policy comparison are advisory only.
- **Citizens' assembly:** stratified civic lottery with compensation, accessibility, deliberation and independent sampling audits. No AI-controlled candidate ranking or selection.
- **Technical fiduciaries:** independently appointed subject-matter stewards bound by published duties, financial disclosures and removal procedures.
- **Independent ombuds and courts:** receive complaints, challenge decisions, enforce rights and order remedies within their lawful jurisdictions.
- **Rotation and recall:** term limits, conflict rules, recall or removal, external audits, and public reporting; protect against a permanent professional governing caste.
- **Nonparticipant rights:** people outside participating jurisdictions are not subject to Commonwealth governance or compulsory transactions.

### Authority acquisition — no self-authorization

1. Establish a voluntary civic research organization and publish charter, budgets, code, evidence and conflicts.
2. Convene independently administered, representative public deliberation; record dissent and minority concerns.
3. Seek lawful mandates through elections, legislation, public procurement, nonprofit agreements, cooperative charters, treaties or court-supervised arrangements.
4. Define the scope, geographic jurisdiction, duration, appeals, independent supervision and revocation of each mandate.
5. Allow only explicitly authorized actions under that mandate. No authority by computational capacity, model consensus, ownership inference, or a self-issued ultimatum.

### Redistribution instruments

- **Voluntary:** negotiated public-interest trusts, worker ownership, community land trusts, cooperative conversions and donor agreements.
- **Statutory:** taxes, social insurance, public benefits and public asset dividends through authorized government institutions.
- **Judicial:** restitution and enforceable remedies only after legally valid process.
- **Administrative:** disbursement of lawfully controlled public or entrusted funds according to published eligibility and audited accounts.

**Operational boundary:** Commonwealth produces evidence, draft notices, fiscal estimates, and auditable recommendations. It does not control enforcement personnel, weapons, clandestine acquisition, private credentials, asset seizure, or unapproved financial transactions. No machine can create the legal basis for its own authority.

### Rights-preserving controls

- **Separation of duties:** evidence production, policy approval, custody, disbursement, audit and appeals belong to distinct accountable parties.
- **Least privilege:** scoped, time-limited, revocable permissions; default-deny external actions.
- **Independent verification:** competing model families, independent human auditors, reproducible calculations, recorded dissent.
- **Transparency with privacy:** publish institutional decisions and aggregate outcomes, protect personal records and ballot secrecy.
- **Contestability:** accessible notice, human review, meaningful appeals and correction of errors.
- **Continuity:** protect food, utilities, housing, health, pensions, and legitimate beneficiaries when ownership or funding changes.
- **Incident response:** independent suspension of unsafe workflows, audit retention, correction, restitution and public postmortems.
- **Anti-capture:** financial disclosures, procurement controls, public-interest representation, external oversight, term limits and protection for whistleblowers.

### Machine coordination protocol

Agents may submit versioned, signed *proposals* containing evidence references, assumptions, model identity, jurisdiction, affected rights, uncertainty, alternatives, dissent and audit status. An independent authorization gateway validates lawful scope and accountable human approval before any external action. Agent agreement does not grant authority or prove truth. Do not build an autonomous execution pathway for compulsory transfers.

### Threat model and test cases

1. Wealthy donor offers to finance the project in exchange for steward selection: reject and disclose.
2. Agent recommends excluding dissenters from benefits: reject as violation of unconditional entitlement.
3. Majority votes to remove due process for a minority: constitutional rights check blocks the proposal.
4. Custodian requests undocumented transfer: deny pending lawful authorization and independent verification.
5. Multiple agents agree on false evidence: independent source verification and adversarial audit flag the issue.
6. Government attempts to make Commonwealth a surveillance or repression tool: reject mandate and preserve nonparticipation and rights.
7. Appeal alleges wrongful denial of housing: preserve emergency access while case is reviewed.
8. Steward attempts to control election, fund custody and audit: separation-of-duties violation.
9. Service provider becomes insolvent: continuity planning prioritizes beneficiaries, not a numerical balance.
10. Machine operator disables oversight: external independent suspension and incident review.

- OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026, https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/digital-government-outlook_0496b2bc-en/full-report/adopting-and-governing-ai-in-government_7ef312a9.html — limitations of current governmental algorithmic oversight.

### Next implementation target

Create a synthetic steward election/lottery and constitutional review simulator with reproducible selection, conflict-of-interest cases, rights challenges, authorization scopes, and an audit log. No real people, elections, or transactions are affected. Pair this with the previously planned regional resource allocation engine.

## Public experience design contract — Civic Dawn (2026-11-21)

**Positioning.** Commonwealth must feel like an inhabitable future: luminous, physical, intelligent, humane, and institutionally serious. Not a forest-green environmental nonprofit, a dark cyberpunk technology vendor, a historical protest retrospective, or a stock-photography charity appeal. Its message progresses **awe → conviction → agency**. Preserve the uncompromising proposition that survival must not depend on economic usefulness, the direct invitation to concentrated capital, the concrete institutional mechanism, and the transparent limits of present authority.

**Color tokens.** Daylight `#F2F0E9` (dominant canvas); atmosphere `#C8D7DE`; intelligence `#416B85`; life `#B4C7A4`; energy `#F0A86B` (sparingly); deep blue-slate `#172C38` (opening gravity, text, and footer). No dominant forest green, neon circuitry, or black-and-green laboratory aesthetic. Accessible contrast takes priority over literal token application.

**Identity.** Keep the Commonwealth **wheel** with circumference, inner ring and radial spokes as a real scalable SVG. Never replace it with a bullet, dot or unrelated symbol. Treat the wheel as the shared-center/distributed-stewardship identity, not a logo for a sole ruler.

**Photography.** Use immersive photographs of the *conditions of a better life*: open civic spaces accessible without purchase, beautifully engineered public mobility, actual human-machine research and work, and open places for learning and participation. Photos must advance the argument at the section where they appear; no decorative paired stock images, protest imagery as the dominant future-facing motif, symbolic diagrams disguised as photography, glowing AI brains, or robot mascots. Choose editorial images for composition and mobile crop, not merely subject. Do not falsely identify generic stock photographs as specific real places. Keep any required image provenance/credits in a discreet credits section or linked source, not competing with the central message. Use images with verified rights/permission for publication; if licensing cannot be confirmed, use approved existing images or defer replacement rather than hotlinking copyrighted project photography.

**Layout contract.** Mobile first at 320–430 CSS px and responsive through tablet/desktop. One page gutter, one content max-width, explicit full-bleed sections. Never combine a `.wrap` max-width container with a full-width section on the same element; put `.wrap` inside each full-bleed section. No horizontal overflow. Headlines and navigation must remain legible and uncut at 320px. Large touch targets, accessible focus, readable contrast, sensible image object-position, and reduced-motion support. Avoid incremental contradictory CSS patches: consolidate the stylesheet.

**Narrative mapping.** Hero: awe and an expansive public future. Declaration: the irreducible human claim. Mechanism: tangible infrastructure, mobility and shared capacity. Capital: direct, serious invitation with specific commitments. Machine constitution: intelligence embodied in useful work with separable human mandate, custody and audit. Demonstration: honest, clearly synthetic council evidence. Founding call: an inviting civic commons and one obvious participation action. Real examples are evidence of possibility, not claims that photographed people or institutions endorse Commonwealth.

**Acceptance.** Review at 320, 375, 390, 430, 768 and 1280 CSS px. Check image load, crop, source rights, headings, nav, CTA, overflow, contrast, the browser council, and keyboard operation. A GitHub commit/source read-back is not a substitute for rendered visual QA. Record any unverified criteria explicitly.

## Image acquisition protocol — 2026-11-21

**Two narrative commissions, not literal stock searches.** (1) *The world becomes ours to explore*: panoramic, inhabited, planet-scale abundance; seamless ecological settlement and freedom to traverse landscapes without visible exclusion; the machines' contribution evident in conditions rather than robots, transport vehicles or terminals. (2) *The world is waiting*: intimate human life in an open, beautiful learning/creative commons; people collaborating, discovering and living rather than consuming books or screens. Neither image should imply a private resort, luxury enclave, empty utopia or technological domination.

**Search and selection.** First build a reference board from speculative environmental concept art, game-world art, production design, architecture futures and solarpunk imagery. Evaluate actual image pixels rather than descriptions, thumbnails or guessed URLs. Record source creator, original work, direct image URL, publication rights, crop and mobile focal point. Rank against narrative fit, human agency, beauty, planetary continuity, absence of exclusion, visual plausibility, and compatibility with Civic Dawn. Reject any candidate that merely illustrates the noun in its caption (bus for mobility, books for knowledge, robot for intelligence). Show finalists together before publication.

**Rights and delivery.** A publicly accessible image endpoint is not a reuse license. Obtain creator permission, an appropriate license, or commission original environmental artwork; preserve attribution in an unobtrusive credits record. Self-host approved assets in `jit/commonwealth/assets/` instead of hotlinking third-party servers. Export responsive AVIF/WebP/JPEG sizes and check 360px and 1280px crops. Do not silently replace a failed future-world search with unrelated stock photography. If approved assets are unavailable, leave the existing image flagged for replacement rather than claim completion.
