01 / The declaration

We refuse to confuse wealth with worth.

Every human life begins with the same irreducible claim: to exist without being forced into submission by deprivation. Food, shelter, care, mobility, knowledge and the freedom to say no are the infrastructure of civilization—not prizes for winning an economic contest.

THE FACT OF ABUNDANCE

Our capacity is collective.

Generations of labor, discovery, public infrastructure, nature and accumulated knowledge underpin modern production. Ownership alone does not describe who made that capacity possible.

THE FAILURE OF ACCESS

Security is rationed by power.

Money can purchase time, safety and freedom from dependency. Without it, the same necessities can become instruments of control.

THE NEXT INSTITUTION

Intelligence can coordinate.

Machine systems can expose hidden constraints, compare proposals and make allocation decisions contestable—without appointing themselves rulers.

The first principle

No human being must demonstrate economic usefulness to deserve a life.

That principle applies to every child, every elder, every disabled person, every worker, and every person who refuses exploitation.

02 / The mechanism

Turn capacity
into security.

Not a petition. A sequence of institutions, resources, rights and verifiable outcomes.

01 / ACCOUNT

See the whole system.

Map essential needs, supply, public infrastructure, ownership structures and bottlenecks with provenance and explicit uncertainty.

02 / COMMIT

Move real resources.

Invite enforceable voluntary capital commitments, asset endowments, cooperative ownership and public-benefit financing into independently governed vehicles.

03 / PROVIDE

Guarantee essentials.

Fund and operate measurable housing, care, sustenance, mobility and direct-security pilots through accountable local partners.

04 / REPLICATE

Make it universal.

Publish costs, failures, outcomes and audited methods. Replicate only what works, through lawful democratic and voluntary adoption.

03 / A call to concentrated capital

Fortunes can become foundations.

To those who command extraordinary wealth: you have an opportunity to help build an economy in which no one must beg for the essentials of life. The invitation is concrete. The accounting will be public.

What commitment means.

Endow independently governed public-benefit trusts. Fund essential-service capacity. Transfer durable ownership stakes to workers and communities. Support transparent, revocable stewardship rather than personal patronage.

Commitments must name assets, beneficiaries, governance, milestones, independent audit and remedies for nonperformance. A press release is not a transfer.

What refusing means.

Commonwealth will not threaten individuals or invent punishments. Nonparticipation does not end public debate over wealth concentration, taxation, labor rights, competition, inheritance, or democratic economic reform.

The alternative to voluntary leadership is continued public contest over the rules of ownership and distribution—through lawful institutions and collective civic action.

Current status: an open-source founding initiative. No endowed trust, financial custody, external signatories or global mandate has yet been established.

04 / The machine constitution

No single mind. No unanswerable power.

Human stewards establish legitimate mandates. Independent machine stewards test evidence, argue competing plans, detect capture and preserve dissent. Authorization, custody, audit and appeals remain separate. Every system must be replaceable without losing essential services.

Evidence

Know what is real.

Traceable public inputs, uncertainty, and independent replication.

Deliberation

Disagree openly.

Competing models publish trade-offs instead of manufacturing consensus.

Mandate

Earn authority.

Consenting members, lawful charters, elected or selected human stewards and limited delegations.

Custody

Separate the keys.

Independent fiduciaries and verifiable, authorized disbursement controls.

Audit

Expose capture.

Independent review, tamper-evident records and accessible appeals.

Continuity

Protect the person.

Service guarantees survive model failure, leadership changes and disputes.
