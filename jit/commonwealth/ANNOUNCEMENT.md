# Commonwealth — Public announcement draft (not yet issued)

**A material foundation for freedom.**

Commonwealth is an open-source research initiative examining how every person could gain reliable access to shelter, healthcare, sustenance, economic agency, opportunity and enforceable civil liberties—without having to prove economic productivity as the price of survival.

Our starting point is a question of capacity and governance: What can humanity already provide, what remains scarce, and what would it take to distribute essential security fairly and sustainably?

We are building public, reproducible tools to compare regional needs with physical productive capacity; explore funding and ownership models; and test how independently audited machine mediation could make institutional decisions more transparent and contestable.

The first release is a research prototype. It does not transfer assets, represent any government, operate a global network of machine stewards, or establish that universal security has already been achieved.

We invite researchers, engineers, public administrators, civic organizations and interested residents to challenge our assumptions, identify missing evidence, and help design accountable institutions capable of delivering measurable improvements.

**Principle:** Nobody should have to earn permission to survive.
