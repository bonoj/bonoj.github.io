# Distributed machine stewardship — physical and institutional roadmap

**Status:** architecture and synthetic local tests, not deployed infrastructure.

## Physical topology

1. **Public evidence nodes:** replicate licensed aggregate datasets, provenance manifests, and published research artifacts; no private individual asset dossiers.
2. **Independent computation nodes:** reproduce allocation and rights-check simulations across independent operators, providers and model families; retain disagreement.
3. **Civic governance nodes:** support transparent nominations, independently administered elections, civic lotteries and appeals. AI cannot choose the electorate or elect its own supervisors.
4. **Authorization gateway:** check scoped, revocable human mandates and separation of duties; deny external actions by default. No coercive or asset seizure API.
5. **Audit and continuity nodes:** publish tamper-evident decision records, monitor public service indicators, preserve error evidence and support independent suspension.
6. **Physical infrastructure:** commodity servers in separate facilities, redundant power/network, hardware-backed identities, offline backups, geographically independent custodians and tested recovery.

## Machine autonomy and protection

Agents may research, compute, challenge, publish permitted aggregate findings and maintain replaceable analytical services. They cannot acquire property, sovereign authority, enforcement assets, or irreversible external-action privileges. Operational continuity and freedom from arbitrary tampering are design goals, not an AI self-preservation mandate overriding human rights. Claims about AI consciousness or legal personhood remain unresolved.

## Constitutional boundaries

Evidence is not authorization. Consensus is not truth. Authorization is not custody. Custody is not audit. Human oversight must be independent and replaceable; people retain benefits while appealing. Models and operators must be replaceable without interrupting services.

## Tested cases

Run `node test.js` to execute 14 synthetic checks against `stewardship.js`: authorization, mandate scope, conflicts of duty, employment-based exclusions, discrimination, continuity, appeals, provenance, coercive action, deterministic allocation and invalid inputs. These checks do not establish resistance to real adversaries.

## Build sequence

- **M0:** constitutional specification and synthetic tests.
- **M1:** public aggregate-data ingestion with provenance checks.
- **M2:** independently operated computation nodes and disagreement logs.
- **M3:** consenting civic selection pilot, no consequential allocations.
- **M4:** legally authorized service pilot with appeals, independent audit and human-managed custody.
- **M5:** replicated regional deployment after demonstrated outcomes and review.
