# LinksIn

Standalone JIT: https://bonoj.github.io/jit/linksin/

A fictional professional network where chain-link fences seek properties to enclose. Satirical imitation of professional-networking interface conventions: blue-and-white visual language, feed, profiles, property opportunities, endorsements, and connection requests. Not affiliated with LinkedIn.

Single self-contained mobile-responsive HTML file. All posts and interactions are local to the browser session; no account, server, personal data collection, or real job applications.

## Interaction

- Home feed with fence career announcements and thought leadership.
- Like, repost, comment, and publish local posts.
- Browse properties and express interest in enclosure opportunities.
- Network and notification interactions.

## Creative direction

Fences are the professionals. Properties are the employers. Every business-network cliché is taken literally. Avoid explaining the joke within the fictional product experience.
