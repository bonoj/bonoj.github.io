# LinksIn

https://bonoj.github.io/jit/linksin/

A fictional professional network for chain-link fences seeking properties to enclose. A satirical imitation of professional-networking culture, built as a standalone JIT. Not affiliated with LinkedIn.

## World

The fences are the professionals, properties are employers, and the economy is organized around enclosure. Characters have recurring perspectives, rivalries, and career histories. The public experience stays entirely in character.

## Runtime

- Single mobile-responsive `index.html`; no build system, backend, account, user submissions, or data collection.
- 14 recurring characters, each with authored story beats and characteristic opinions.
- Seeded deterministic feed generator mixing character-specific stories, locations, claims, and endings. Duplicate authored combinations are tracked during the session.
- 24 posts at initialization, 9 more added as the reader approaches the bottom, up to 600 posts; manual "Show more" adds 12.
- 45 notifications at initialization, then 1–3 additional notifications every 4.5 seconds while the page is visible; unread count increases over time. Older notifications are pruned from the DOM.
- Comment threads reveal in-universe replies. Like, follow, repost, and share affordances respond locally.
- Property listings with local expression-of-interest feedback.
- No ability to publish posts; the site is meant to be consumed as an absurd, endlessly busy network.
- 14 distinct Wikimedia Commons photo file references in rotation. Images load directly from Wikimedia via Special:FilePath and may be subject to Commons rate limits and source availability.

## Art direction

Dense and familiar professional-network visual language. Actual chain-link fence photographs, not emoji avatars or procedurally drawn fence substitutes. Overwhelming professional self-importance, petty rivalries, constant notifications, and complete sincerity.
