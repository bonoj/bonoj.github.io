# ⚙️ Ender Forge — Genesis Chronicle
*19 October 2026 · Genesis*

## Why this world exists
**Can an interpreting intelligence, situated in a persistent executable world, develop increasingly effective judgment through experimentation, observation and accumulated knowledge?**

Ender Forge exists to explore that question, not to prescribe the intelligence's personality, trajectory or eventual capabilities. Persistent context, tools and evidence may help successive model invocations build upon verified discoveries without changing model weights. Whether and how that works remains an experimental question.

Ender Forge begins as a gameplay clone of the known-working Evermore II world, subsequently given its own JIT identity and handoff. The inherited ecology is **experimental substrate**, not the Forge's purpose. Do not restart or redesign it merely to establish a new laboratory.

## 🗝️ Semantomancy — the interface
**Semantomancy** is the practice of directing complex computational and creative systems through compact, meaning-rich expressions whose operational significance is established by shared context, persistent knowledge and executable machinery. Its mechanism is **semantic compression coupled with contextual reconstruction**.

- **📜 The Five Principles:** a true name invokes shared semantics; a magically sorted library preserves the substance beyond a lightweight scroll; **🧞 the Djinn's Lamp** represents interpretive agency, not infallibility; **🌀 pocket dimensions** are real executable worlds reached through metaphorical doors; **⚖️ the law of consequence** requires evidence, constraints and human agency.
- **⚗️ Nature and limits:** this is neither supernatural nor a new computational primitive. Unlike a fixed macro, interpretation adapts to context. Unlike an isolated prompt, established agreements and institutional memory can be recovered. It remains fallible.
- **⚙️ Demonstration:** after a GitHub Chronicle write failed, the human invoked *Sanctum Contract*; the model recovered an existing blob → tree → commit → ref procedure and verified the read-back. The model **did not retrieve that procedure independently** on the first failures. Knowledge persistence and timely retrieval are different capabilities.
- **✨ Proposition:** semantic compression does not abolish complexity; **it gives complexity an address**. The measure is reliable, intelligible, verifiable consequence, not incantation length.

*The semantomancer carries no tower upon their back. Only a scroll, a lamp, and the names of doors.*

## 🧠 The Conjoiner relationship
The human brings intent, experience, taste, observation, imagination and final agency. The model brings synthesis, critique, proposals, implementation and technical judgment. Neither is confined to a designer/coder role. The executable world provides evidence and unforeseen consequences, not consciousness. **Mutual intelligibility matters more than maximum autonomy.** Surprise belongs in invention, never in concealed consequences or invented verification.

The institution has layers: **Sanctum** for orientation, collaboration and recovery; **README** for current laws and cold boot; **Chronicle** for causation, experiments and decisions; **source and Git history** for what changed; **published browser and 👁** for what executed. ## 🌱 Working understanding — Conjoiner growth (19 October 2026)
**Status: evolving interpretation, not a product specification or demonstrated outcome.** The conversation moved from cultivating an exceptionally capable model toward **empowering a human through an external mind extension**. The working phrase is **Conjoiner brain**: not a digital twin, not a replica of the human, and not a replacement for their judgment.

The human can be anyone. “Child” names a posture of curiosity and becoming, not an age limit. Youth may be an especially meaningful audience, but the possibility extends to any person entering an unfamiliar field or imagining beyond their present tools.

The human contributes lived experience, purpose, taste, curiosity and agency. The external brain contributes another perspective, reasoning, invention, technical reach and continuity through recoverable knowledge and artifacts. Their **conjunction** may become more capable over time. *Conjoiner growth* describes expanding shared capability; *Conjoiner plasticity* is a tentative term for how the arrangement can reorganize as new questions, instruments, vocabulary and experiences accumulate. Neither term implies model-weight updates or measured changes in human neuroplasticity.

**Executable authority** is central: the external brain can help instantiate and alter bounded worlds rather than only discuss them. **Cheap failure and simple recovery** let experiments go wrong without losing the work. Imagination can remain open while consequences stay inspectable and grounded. Authority outside recoverable digital sandboxes—especially for youth—would require explicit limits, privacy protections and appropriate human oversight.

The desired outcome is **human empowerment**, not maximum assistant autonomy, dependence, or a factory producing standardized people. A useful conjunction should enlarge what the human can understand, create and attempt, ideally strengthening independent judgment as well. Whether this happens, and for whom, must be discovered rather than assumed.

The lineage is visible but not prescriptive: **Hermeticon** supplies a method for executable inquiry; **Evermore II** supplies a working ecology of consequential interactions; **Ender Forge** is a place to investigate how a human and an interpreting external brain might grow in capability together. The **Hermeticon Institute** may eventually let others encounter such work, but its public Sanctum remains a separate, unimplemented design direction.

### Possible crafts and future vocabulary
The human imagined **cyberpilot**, **cybercontroller**, and **semantomancer** as possible job titles in a world where human–model conjunctions become ordinary. These are **speculative names for emerging practices**, not established occupations, fixed roles, a taxonomy, or a forecast.

A *cyberpilot* might steer complex intelligent or executable systems without manually directing every part. A *cybercontroller* might focus on their state, coordination, boundaries, evidence and accountable intervention. A *semantomancer* might use language, shared context and recoverable executable machinery to invoke, compose, test and evolve capabilities. Their boundaries may overlap, dissolve or prove unhelpful; **Conjoiner** could describe a broader relationship rather than another credential.

One possibility is that accessible external cognitive machinery shifts some expertise from memorizing implementation details toward asking incisive questions, exercising judgment, imagining structures, understanding systems, and verifying consequences. It need not erase technical depth; it may change who can begin, how they learn, and what new forms of mastery emerge.

These titles are invitations to notice nascent crafts, **not doctrine to teach or identities to impose**. First explore what people actually do with the Forge. Let useful language follow the practice.

Do not freeze this understanding into a curriculum, named developmental stages, agent persona, or mandatory architecture. Revise it as experiments and conversation sharpen the question.

### Method: a situated expedition, not an omniscient agent

Tiffany and her ecology continue independently; no knowledge or resources are silently transferred to Ender. A separate 👁 developer digest can describe global conditions for **the experimenters** but must never be attributed to Ender's senses.

For every encounter, retain the timestamp, position, cumulative distance, exact entity kinds and relative geometry, sensory limits, and the distinction between observation and inference. A missing entity means only **not reported within this snapshot's radius**, not absent from the world. Relative offsets are measured from Ender's position at that moment; successive snapshots are not fixed-camera maps. Cumulative `steps` is path length, **not** displacement. We cannot reconstruct the path or infer what Ender passed between samples. Hypotheses about ecology, intent or causation require repeat observations and interventions, not a single suggestive arrangement.

**Critical continuity distinction:** the model and Chronicle can remember all reports across turns; **Ender's running code currently cannot**. He has no encounter history, learned map, temporal recognition, purposeful action, or persistent cognition. A future bounded memory should distinguish previously witnessed evidence (with last-seen time and location) from what is visible now, without retroactively treating the following human-curated history as Ender's internal knowledge.

## Architectural pivot — from authored curiosity to model-mediated agency (19 October 2026)

### Evidence that prompted reconsideration

The first live cognitive-v2 report at **T+129** showed Ender at **(709,457)**, **2,456 units walked**, **43 samples**, and only three moth workers plus a moth depot currently in view. The new system successfully emitted bounded local cargo-count changes, preserved landmark memory, maintained goals over multiple sampling intervals, and recorded arrival outcomes. The report also retained two nearly identical 💧 tower records at (667,455) and (668,455), revealing position-quantization identity instability.

The critical failure was epistemic rather than syntactic: Ender could arrive at a moth or crab depot and record `arrived — outcome still uncertain`, even when a matching depot was visible. Cargo-event locations were the position of Ender when sampling, not identified worker trajectories; chasing those coordinates would not establish cargo behavior. Our response was about to prescribe still more event classifiers, investigation completion rules, uncertainty phrases, and diary language.

**The human challenged the premise:** “Are we adding too many words to Ender's mouth? Should we just let him evaluate as a lightweight model?” The model agrees. The current `ender-mind/v2` is a useful **instrumented baseline**, but extending it into an ever-larger authored approximation of curiosity risks making the human/model developers the actual authors of Ender's questions, goals, reasons and apparent discoveries. More rules can make a diary sound intelligent without granting the agent meaningful latitude to decide what matters.

### LanternSandbox inspection — concrete prior art

At the human's direction, the model inspected the existing [bonoj/LanternSandbox](https://github.com/bonoj/LanternSandbox) repository (default branch `main`), a Unity/C# experiment in model-mediated agents. Its root is a Unity project (Assets, Packages, ProjectSettings), not a directly embeddable browser inference engine. The following source files were inspected:

- [`Assets/LLMClient.cs`](https://github.com/bonoj/LanternSandbox/blob/main/Assets/LLMClient.cs) sends OpenAI-compatible `/v1/chat/completions` requests using UnityWebRequest, with configurable endpoint defaulting to `http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1/chat/completions`, model identifier `qwen2.5`, explicit system/user messages and JSON response parsing. This is a **transport example**, not proof that inference is available to a GitHub Pages page or on the human's phone.
- [`Assets/LLMDecisionSource.cs`](https://github.com/bonoj/LanternSandbox/blob/main/Assets/LLMDecisionSource.cs) composes contextual facts and an `OPTIONS_JSON` action list, requests a constrained `{"key":string,"reason":string}` answer, and hands the parsed decision to its caller.
- [`Assets/DecisionMenuBuilder.cs`](https://github.com/bonoj/LanternSandbox/blob/main/Assets/DecisionMenuBuilder.cs) aggregates `IOptionProvider` contributions. Its `DecisionOption` distinguishes model-visible keys/descriptions/facts from nonserialized engine-only data. `IExecutionSystem` separately tests whether a decision can execute and applies it; `IContextProvider` and `IOptionDecorator` keep context construction composable.
- [`Assets/LLMAgent.cs`](https://github.com/bonoj/LanternSandbox/blob/main/Assets/LLMAgent.cs) demonstrates an agent requesting one of the offered actions, awaiting a response, and passing a parsed decision back to the engine.
- [`Assets/StoryExperiment/Scripts/Summarizer.cs`](https://github.com/bonoj/LanternSandbox/blob/main/Assets/StoryExperiment/Scripts/Summarizer.cs) demonstrates separate model-mediated condensation of narrative events and stated intentions into a compact chapter, with an explicit prohibition on inventing events.

**Transferable design principle:** The simulation owns physical truth, observations, available affordances and execution; a model interprets bounded evidence, selects among permitted actions and can author concise grounded notes. Context, options, model transport, validation, execution and memory condensation are separate components. The model must never acquire Tiffany's hidden global state merely because the developer observer can see it.

### Decision and rationale

**Pivot Ender's next frontier to a lightweight-model-mediated mind, drawing on LanternSandbox's contracts.** Stop expanding handcrafted question templates, priority heuristics, goal explanations and diary prose as the primary cognitive architecture. Preserve the current `ender-mind/v2` runtime **unchanged for now** as a working, observable fallback and comparison baseline; do not remove it before a new path is specified, tested and demonstrated. No new model integration was implemented by this Chronicle entry.

Target architecture:

1. **Body / world:** Existing Ender movement, collision, sensor radius and Tiffany world remain authoritative and deterministic. Ender still begins with no tower, resources or special permissions.
2. **Perception / memory:** Deliver only genuinely local evidence and bounded, lossy history; distinguish observation, inference and unknowns. Use a separate summarization/compaction contract if needed.
3. **Mind:** A lightweight model may formulate its own questions, choose what deserves attention, propose goals and write brief field notes, instead of being supplied predetermined sentences masquerading as its thoughts.
4. **Affordance menu / interpreter:** Offer a small, explicit, validated action vocabulary (e.g. explore, travel to a locally known destination, observe/wait). Model output must select a valid key; engine resolves destinations, validates feasibility and executes physically. Do not permit invented coordinates, teleportation, unearned powers or unsupported world mutations.
5. **Transport / fallback:** Determine how inference is actually provided to the mobile browser. Lantern's loopback Unity server cannot simply be copied into a public HTTPS GitHub Pages deployment. Compare feasible local/on-device and service-backed inference with explicit attention to browser compatibility, HTTPS/CORS, latency, power, memory, privacy and key handling. Do not embed secret credentials in public client code. If inference is absent or fails, preserve safe deterministic wandering/observation rather than claiming a model is thinking.

### Next work and proof standard

Inspect LanternSandbox's remaining **agent controller, context providers, option providers and execution systems** before finalizing the adapter contract. Then revise [`SPEC.md`](./SPEC.md) around the model-mediated architecture, preserving the existing behavioral and safety invariants. Select and verify a concrete inference route **before** wiring live model calls into Ender Forge. Build one narrow vertical slice: bounded local observation → compact context + executable options → model decision → strict validation → world action → subsequent observed consequence. Add model-authored notes only once they can be distinguished from raw facts and checked against evidence. Test malformed output, missing model, timeout, stale decisions, invalid keys, repeated goals and loss of connectivity.

**Success is not a more eloquent diary.** Success is Ender choosing an investigation that was *not authored by a fixed heuristic*, executing a permitted action, and revising a later decision in light of the observed outcome. Do not claim subjective experience, autonomous causal learning or live inference before demonstration.

**Status:** Pivot decided and recorded. Existing Ender runtime retained. Lantern architecture inspected at the named source files; deeper controller/adapter inspection and inference selection remain pending.

## Pivot deep dive: from telemetry to an evidence-bearing agent (19 October 2026)

**Decision and rationale.** The human asked whether the observation program was ambitious enough. The model's conclusion was that instrumentation had succeeded at its immediate task but risked becoming an end in itself. The observed T+42/T+94 reports establish real local combat and economic transitions, honest uncertainty and a fixed read-only counterfactual. They do **not** establish learning, individual recognition, reliable causal closure or informed agency. Further expanding event counts and categories alone would optimize measurement rather than move Ender toward becoming an independent participant. The human and model therefore advanced the earlier observation-stop decision into a **five-development-turn agency roadmap**: by Turn 5, Ender must take an actual action in the world because of an inspectable choice grounded in accumulated evidence.

**Why identity changes the design.** A worker-following feature that only matches `ant-worker` by kind or nearest position is brittle: two ants can cross, one can die and respawn, a depot can relocate, and Ender can leave sensing range. False continuity would corrupt every downstream conclusion. Instead, Ender should be able to tag *any encountered entity*: workers, towers, depots, resources, and terrain sites. An Ender-local tag is a memory handle, **not a global tracking capability**. A live worker token can be associated with the same runtime actor during local encounters, but current state is accessible only when within the 125-unit sensing boundary. Static features require conservative matching and can become identity-uncertain after relocation. Actor replacement is a new identity, not a resurrection of the previous worker. Memory persists within a bounded session even when visibility ends, with last-seen location and uncertainty rather than secret offscreen coordinates. Generalizing identity early prevents one-off machinery for each new behavior.

**Why evidence must precede inference.** The existing semantic emitter knows when an ant or crab actually picks up or deposits material. Ender should store a bounded provenance-bearing relationship only when it directly receives the locally permitted transition or an unambiguous observation. `Ant A carried wood` and `Depot D received wood` are separate facts; `Ant A delivered wood to Depot D` requires evidence connecting A, D and the credited deposit. A deposit by a second ant of the same kind cannot close A's question. Distinguish observed facts from hypotheses, unknowns, contradictions and stale memories. The eventual cognition is a small revisable model of individuals, interactions and dependencies, **not** a fictional inner monologue or a predetermined explanation of Tiffany's economy.

**Why follow is a stepping stone rather than the goal.** The first investigation loop tests whether a question can alter movement and close against real evidence: witness a carrier → select that particular tagged actor → follow within normal locomotion and locality constraints → witness a correctly associated delivery, or honestly record lost/ambiguous/timed-out/danger/interrupt. The existing `enderUpdate(dt)` remains the sole movement authority (speed 19 world units/s, boundary/tower/sanctuary avoidance); the cognition layer supplies bounded goals, not direct teleportation or world manipulation. One reversible switch must restore the prior heuristic behavior. The fixed 16-feature shadow remains read-only and does not gain policy authority, reward or training by implication. Existing 🧠 diary/export is sufficient; no new controls or HUD.

**Why Turn 5 is a stronger test.** A hardcoded follow action is not enough. Ender must remember a witnessed relationship, later face at least two legitimate choices (for example, revisit a depot that received cargo versus explore an unfamiliar site), select one *because of its evidence*, and physically navigate there. The export must expose candidate actions, supporting evidence, expected result, rejected alternative, chosen goal, motion and actual outcome or uncertainty. A counterfactual fixture should alter the remembered evidence and show a different selected action. Browser observation must confirm actual movement; model prediction alone is insufficient. This is informed embodied navigation, **not yet resource harvesting or tower construction**. Material agency and an independently earned one-HP vulnerable tower remain the longer-range objective.

**Five-turn implementation sequence and exit gates.** (1) **Identity/provenance:** bounded entity registry, local-only ephemeral actor identity, fallible static matching; test two same-kind workers, respawn, visibility loss, relocation, reset and memory bounds. (2) **Relationships:** evidence-backed worker/cargo/depot links with explicit unknowns and contradictions; test correct versus wrong-worker deposits. (3) **Investigation:** reversible, committed follow-carrier behavior with honest completion, loss and timeout; test navigation constraints and no omniscient pursuit. (4) **Choice:** inspectable alternatives and evidence-weighted choice; demonstrate different evidence yields different action, with the fixed shadow still read-only. (5) **Action:** live browser proof of a remembered delivery influencing a subsequent chosen destination and physical movement; repeat under changed evidence, check rollback and Tiffany invariants. If Turn 5's acceptance evidence is missing, report it as incomplete rather than declaring victory.

**Architectural constraints and deliberate deferrals.** Keep Ender's sensing at radius 125, no replay of unseen remote events, finite event/identity/relationship/goal collections, one active investigation, conservative actor association, and no external privileged world queries. Preserve Tiffany's autonomous workers, exclusive material claims, tower recovery, combat, economy, one-page mobile presentation, and the immutable `jit/evermore-2/` baseline. Do not add a second movement engine, new UI, unearned resources, combat or tower powers, or silently train/steer via the shadow. The model's planning turn produced the roadmap in `SPEC.md` (commit `943278aa`); **no runtime implementation has begun**, so the next engineering turn is **Turn 1**, not Turn 2. Each implementation turn must commit/read back source, run scoped regression checks, distinguish Pages publication from executing-browser verification, and log observed outcomes here.

**Documentation contract:** `SPEC.md` is the authoritative design and five-turn acceptance plan; this Chronicle preserves the reasoning and pivot; `README.md` is the short cold-boot handoff. The earlier suggestion to continue event-type discrimination remains optional research, not the active frontier.
