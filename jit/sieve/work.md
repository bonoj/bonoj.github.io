## Qualification rubric
For each opportunity record:
- **Decision maker / funded opening:** who specifically can approve payment?
- **Money signal:** what verifiable evidence shows budget, hiring, sponsorship, procurement, or paid demand **now**?
- **Time to income:** how much process stands between us and payment?
- **Autonomy and energy cost:** what would the arrangement demand of John?
- **Fit and evidence:** which existing artifact or capability is genuinely relevant?
- **Next action and deadline:** one concrete step, with source links and dated verification.
- **Confidence:** separate facts from hypotheses and unknowns.

Rank by plausible **time-to-stable-income, probability, autonomy, and cognitive cost**, not prestige or novelty. Warm contacts and funded openings generally beat broad speculative cold emails. A genuine role may beat a tiny project. An actual sponsor may beat both.
