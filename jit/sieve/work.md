## Evidence: EUROPA-01 — scientific restraint and a witnessed negative result
**Primary scientific notebook:** https://github.com/bonoj/Ledger/blob/main/notes/EUROPA_01.md
**Executing-world source:** https://github.com/bonoj/Abyss

This is a third flagship proof, complementary to Chemlab and Lattice. The question: what could depth-resolved Europan ice samples reveal about origin, radiation alteration, and possible subsurface exchange, and how could competing explanations be distinguished? The research program separated published observations, synthetic assumptions, hidden simulator truth, instrument-facing measurements, contamination, uncertainty and inference. It built deterministic ice-column experiments, simulated coring, conserved recovered/dispersed material, and connected a truth-blind observation instrument to an executable Europa world.

**Browser-witnessed closing experiment (2026-10-16):** executing Abyss SHA `d9bfea85`; two synthetic cores removed 35 and 49 occupied nodes, with 66 recovered, 18 dispersed and zero ledger residual. Its observed target-minus-reference contrast was -0.023293232872402314 against a predeclared threshold 0.05; **decision: abstain**. The instrument did not resolve the known synthetic histories. This is a legitimate negative result, not evidence about Europa's actual ice composition or ocean.

**Commercial meaning:** The engine can conduct question-first, uncertainty-aware investigations, distinguish simulation truth from observations, instrument real executable behavior, preserve failed hypotheses and null results, and stop when evidence does not justify a claim.
