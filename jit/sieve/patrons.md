# JIT Sieve — Patrons

Updated: 2026-10-22.

## Mission

Find people with **capital, technical imagination, and a demonstrated appetite for unconventional independent research**. Lead with the executable laboratory and intellectual curiosity; **do not quote an asking price**. A patron can propose the financial arrangement after understanding the work.

**Proposition:** An independent, smartphone-operated, model-mediated laboratory for rapidly composing, deploying, observing, and revising executable scientific investigations and interactive worlds. Preserve ownership of preexisting Sanctum/JIT infrastructure and independent inventions. Any commissioned deliverables, exclusivity, or IP rights must be negotiated explicitly.

These are **prospects, not commitments or confirmed invitations**. Public links are for context, not presumed direct contact.

## First encounter

1. Show **one functioning, surprising executable experiment**, not a pitch deck or résumé.
2. Invite the patron to pose an interesting question. Demonstrate how quickly the laboratory can turn it into a reproducible investigation.
3. Explain the reusable machinery: GitHub-native publishing, composable worlds, runtime observation, handoff/chronicle, model-mediated iteration.
4. Ask what research question they wish someone could investigate without assembling a traditional team.
5. Discuss sponsorship structure **only if interest emerges**. Do not anchor a number.
