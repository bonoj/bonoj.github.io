# JIT Sieve — Residence

Updated: 2026-10-22.

## Identity

**Independent Inventor-in-Residence** / **Resident Inventor**.

A self-directed experimental technologist with an independent, model-mediated laboratory for rapidly composing, deploying, observing and revising executable investigations. The residency is a *relationship with a patron*, not necessarily an institutional program, job, cohort, office, or grant.

**Do not name an asking price. Do not wait for funding windows.** Preserve preexisting Sanctum/JIT infrastructure and independent inventions; negotiate any commissioned outputs separately.

## Vocabulary — what to call the arrangement

| Title | What it implies | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent Inventor-in-Residence** | Backed to invent across disciplines, retaining an independent laboratory | **Preferred** |
| **Resident Inventor** | Ongoing patron–inventor relationship without fixed deliverables | **Preferred shorthand** |
| **Researcher-in-Residence** | Investigate and demonstrate new knowledge, usually with a research agenda | Strong if autonomy retained |
| **Technologist-in-Residence** | Cross-domain technical exploration, prototyping, institutional exchange | Strong, but often policy/institutional |
| **Artist-in-Residence** | Protected time and resources for original creative work | Strong for interactive worlds, often limited stipends |
| **Innovator-in-Residence** | Build novel public-facing artifacts, often within a host institution | Relevant, commonly commissioned |
| **Scientist-in-Residence** | Cross-disciplinary scientific inquiry and expert advisory | Relevant but may require formal credentials |
| **Fellow-in-Residence** | Funded research and/or service to a host institution | Conditional |
| **Entrepreneur-in-Residence** | Identify, incubate or found a company | **Usually wrong** unless no company formation requirement |
| **Founder-in-Residence** | Build a venture, usually with equity terms | **Wrong default** |

## Verified examples — models and people, not application deadlines

These are **not all residencies worldwide**, nor are they all suitable. Their purpose is to identify organizations and decision-makers who have **actually supported residents**. An intake window is never a prerequisite to approaching a potential patron.

### A. Serious research / invention residencies

1. **Astera Institute — Residency** — https://astera.org/residency/
   - 12–18 months; salaried $125k–$250k annually and negotiable research budget up to $1.5m; in-person Emeryville; open-science default with negotiable commercial terms.
   - **Fit:** unusually close to inventor support and substantial infrastructure. **Constraint:** institution-directed scientific focus, physical residency, publication/IP negotiation. **Approach:** identify individual research-capital allocator; do not wait for a cohort.
2. **OpenAI — Residency** — https://openai.com/residency/
   - Six months, paid, full-time in San Francisco, AI research apprenticeship; emphasizes self-taught and unconventional builders. Published monthly salary $18,333. 2026 applications closed; start dates can be rolling when hiring.
   - **Fit:** strong recognition of nontraditional technical talent. **Constraint:** an employee-style research training role, not independent patronage. **Approach:** use as evidence of organizational recognition, not as the target structure.
3. **Schmidt Sciences — Fellow in Residence, AI** — https://jobs.lever.co/schmidt-entities/6bef9879-f540-475e-ad26-5254fa4e1ad7
   - New York; 12–18 months, half time independent research and half grantmaking; published $160k annual salary. **Requires recent PhD** and publication credentials.
   - **Fit:** interesting mixed research/funding model. **Constraint:** clear credential mismatch and structured duties. **Approach:** research Eric and Wendy Schmidt's science philanthropy, not this requisition.
4. **Autodesk Research — Residency Program** — https://www.research.autodesk.com/research-areas/residency-program/
   - Academic cohort and industry research network; design, fabrication and experimental practice, with access to Autodesk research resources.
   - **Fit:** interactive tools and simulation could resonate. **Constraint:** project/team and Autodesk-industry alignment, proposal cycles, not necessarily a living stipend. **Approach:** research leaders/hosts as potential partners.
5. **Council on Foreign Relations — Technologist-in-Residence** — https://www.cfr.org/fellowships/technologist-residence-fellowship
   - Technology and national security/foreign policy; 10-month, in-person NYC/DC fellowship; published third-party 2027–28 description says $143,325 salary.
   - **Fit:** unusual overlap of former intelligence analyst and technologist. **Constraint:** policy-focused, office-based, scheduled fellowship; not independent invention.
6. **Library of Congress — Innovator in Residence** — https://blogs.loc.gov/thesignal/2026/03/now-accepting-concepts-for-the-2026-2027-innovator-in-residence-program/
   - Mostly remote; up to $90k/year for up to two years to make public-facing digital works from collections.
   - **Fit:** executable knowledge instruments, public-facing interactive worlds. **Constraint:** institution-specific deliverables and fixed program budget. **Approach:** conceptual model, not target application.
7. **Sanford Underground Research Facility — Artist in Residence** — https://sanfordlab.org/programs/SURFAiR
   - Creative work inspired by underground science; published artist stipend $5k; requires artistic work samples and references.
   - **Fit:** fascinating science/art intersection. **Constraint:** too small and institution-specific for primary patronage.
8. **Cyclotron Road / Activate — Entrepreneurial Science Fellowship** — https://cyclotronroad.lbl.gov/
   - Living stipend, research support and national-lab access for scientist-entrepreneurs.
   - **Fit:** serious research infrastructure. **Constraint:** commercialization/startup trajectory and application cycle.
9. **GovAI — Entrepreneur-in-Residence** — https://www.governance.ai/post/eir
   - One-year salaried position, plus partner seed support for launching AI governance/safety projects; expressions of interest reportedly reviewed on a rolling basis.
   - **Fit:** demonstrates year-round flexible evaluation. **Constraint:** explicitly expects creation of an organization/project in AI governance, not open-ended invention.
10. **Convergent Research — Focused Research Organizations** — https://www.convergentresearch.org/get-involved
    - Not a residency: institutional mechanism to create funded research organizations around bottleneck problems.
    - **Fit:** connects inventive research programs to major funders. **Constraint:** larger, scoped organizations rather than a solo resident inventor.

### B. Programs to deprioritize

- **Startup / founder residencies** such as venture-backed founder fellowships: frequently require company formation, equity, or a predetermined commercial thesis.
- **Unpaid or stipend-light artist residencies**: useful creative networks, not a substitute for substantial independent support.
- **Academic postdoctoral fellowships**: often require PhD, publications, a defined research discipline, and institutional supervision.
- **Application-window programs**: use as evidence of patronage philosophy; do not let their intake schedules control outreach.

## What to offer a patron

**Not:** a resume, hourly rate, grant application, startup deck, or pre-priced package.

**Instead:** a live, inspectable laboratory; a question from the patron; a rapid executable investigation; a conversation about which problems they would explore if the cost of experimentation fell dramatically.

**Proposed relationship:** independent inventor-in-residence; continuous exploratory mandate; periodic demonstrations; optional commissions negotiated separately; protected preexisting IP; no mandatory incorporation; physical presence only if mutually useful.

## Worldwide location filter — Berkeley climate, Boulder heat ceiling

The target is **a patron who permits the inventor to live in a pleasant city**, not necessarily a local institution. Cool summers are favored; cold winters are welcome. Summer heat comparable to or worse than Boulder is a drawback. Avoid equating a residency listing with an unrestricted patronage offer.

**Climate reference:** Berkeley July average high ~74°F (NOAA); Boulder summer highs generally in the 80s. Source: https://www.currentresults.com/Weather/California/city-temperature-july.php and https://www.colorado.edu/isss/scholars/community-resources/boulders-climate .

### Priority hypotheses

1. **Foresight (Allison Duettmann)** is the strongest *new* match: concrete support for small independent AI-for-science builders in two compatible cities, plus evidence of flexible infrastructure. Not a confirmed personal patron.
2. **Schering Foundation / BIFOLD** is the strongest documented **AI + creative worlds residency** outside the US; however it is a defined program, not an open-ended individual arrangement.
3. **Hugo Burge Foundation** is unusually explicit about supporting creators simply to make work in a beautiful, cool setting. Technical mandate and sustained financial scale need testing.
4. **Vancouver, Copenhagen, Wellington, and Nordic capitals** are attractive places to live, but do **not** yet have equally convincing identified discretionary patrons. Do not inflate them into confirmed funding prospects.

### Residency logistics that cannot be assumed

- Right to work / visa sponsorship, especially outside the US.
- Tax residence, health insurance, housing, and whether support is salary, grant, stipend, or personal patronage.
- IP ownership, open publication, independent side projects, and exclusivity.
- Whether physical attendance is mandatory, optional, or not required.
- Whether the patron is able to decide year-round, outside an intake cycle.

## Verification limits

Research reflects publicly available descriptions reviewed 2026-10-22. Program conditions, openings, leadership, and contact routes can change. The list is a **curated landscape**, not an exhaustive global registry or evidence that any listed person has offered patronage. Individual contact routes and decision authority must be verified before outreach.
