# Astra North Star — One Civilization, Three Faces

**Status:** Aspirational design guidance for the Living Institute, not a claim about the current Chaos runtime or a literal image-to-code specification.

**Authority:** Read with `ASTRA_CONJOINER.md` and `ASTRA_LIVING_INSTITUTE_SPEC.md`. Executable Chaos geometry, material conservation, and runtime observations outrank illustrative imagery. The existing three-panel triptych is a composition reference only, **not** an authoritative geometry model. Do not make the Hermeticon into a physical snow globe or a world boundary.

## Vision

Build **one continuous, material, cybernetic biomechanical civilization with three radically different faces**. The same terrain, material BBs, physical world, anatomy contracts, and simulation underlie all views. The Hermeticon is an aperture into this world. Moving the Wanderer or changing camera orientation must not create, destroy, or teleport matter.

The visual north star is a **living Institute whose anatomy is its infrastructure**. Organic, mechanical, and computational systems are physically and causally joined. Detail emerges from functional structure, not cosmetic gears or inert decorative populations.

## The three-panel grammar: Above / Through / Below

### 1. The Crown — a civilization that grows

From above, the pale terrain supports an intricate but readable civilization:

- A monumental **biomechanical observatory**: a functional sensing iris integrated into articulated brass/ceramic spars, BB tissue, tension cables, and real observation behavior; not an eyeball attached to a tower.
- Branching luminous groves, fungal or coral-like colonies, and sensor-bearing populations whose growth uses finite material.
- Living foundries with articulated manipulators, finite material reservoirs, visible fabrication, and process constraints.
- Segmented cybernetic creatures with mineral/BB exoskeletons, functional joints, replaceable components, and locomotion.
- Elevated bridges, cableways, transport networks, surveyors, and autonomous repair creatures.

**Visual principle:** vary density dramatically. A few monumental silhouettes; clusters of extraordinary anatomical richness; open terrain, exposed stone, paths, and negative space. From a distance, silhouettes remain legible; close up, assemblies resolve into meaningful material BB structure.

**Function:** observation, experimentation, fabrication, inhabitation, ecology.

### 2. The Seam — anatomy becomes engineering

The middle view is the **architectural proof**. It reveals the continuous terrain volume and the physical relationships of upper and lower faces, without inventing a separate flat slab, a symmetrical underside wedge, or a geology-defining equatorial ring.

A single organism may extend from an upper sensor grove through anchored structural ribs, tendon cables, conduits, and sidewall mechanisms into a lower root-pump assembly. Other structures wrap around terrain edges or traverse actual openings. The brass equatorial hardware belongs to the Hermeticon, not the terrain.

Show exposed load paths, support anchors, joints, pumps, conductive fibers, structural trusses, articulated counterweights, and service access. Make the physical route of material traceable across boundaries.

**Function:** support, load transfer, material circulation, access, fabrication interfaces, damage and repair.

### 3. The Underside — a civilization that hangs, swims, and reaches

Looking upward, the terrain underside becomes an irregular ceiling for a strikingly different ecology. This is **not** a conventional underground cave, a mirrored surface city, or a globe filled with props.

- Suspended biomechanical leviathans and jellyfish-like organisms with articulated ribs, translucent membranes, responsive tendrils, and finite energy/material needs.
- Segmented crawlers traversing the underside, collecting real debris and servicing actual structures.
- Hanging laboratories and instrument rings supported by cables, rigid spars, and monitored anchors.
- Moving counterweights, rotating sensing arrays, modular platforms, and machines that can be independently suspended or freely mobile.
- Genuine negative space, with a few luminous, exquisitely detailed focal assemblies emerging from darkness.

Detached BBs enter the **existing** debris and capture systems. The viewport aperture does not decide whether debris continues to exist. Physical capture boundaries and visibility must remain distinct.

**Function:** suspended research, retrieval, observation, maintenance, transport, and exploration.

## One material language, many anatomies

All three regions use the same material ownership and transfer contracts. A BB-equivalent may participate in a sensor housing, a structural fitting, a creature's armor, a foundry lining, or a suspended instrument. Distinctiveness comes from composition, morphology, constraints, gradients, density, and behavior — not three unrelated simulation engines.

Required causal chain: **structure → damage → detached debris → recovery → transport → fabrication → new structure**, with atomic ownership transfers, inspectable event receipts, and conserved counts. Grouped or instanced BB structures are acceptable for phone performance; only physically detached material should enter loose BB physics, without duplicating ownership.

## Signature demonstration: the bridge repair

A Crown survey leviathan detects instability in a bridge near a terrain edge. Load sensors register strain; a member fails; finite BB debris falls along the sidewall. A repair arthropod descends an anchored route into the Underside, retrieves recoverable BBs, and delivers them to a suspended foundry organism. The foundry fabricates replacement members. A transport mechanism returns them above. The arthropod repairs the bridge, load redistributes, sensing confirms recovery, and the material ledger reconciles.

This is an **end-to-end target**, not a demand to fake the entire sequence in the first commit. Build verified vertical slices using the existing Chaos systems, then integrate them.

The event should work with the Wanderer absent and remain physically continuous when the camera moves. Sensor → controller → actuator loops must be real, with energy/material costs and observable failures.

## Composability, geometry, and rendering discipline

- This first Astra run remains in **one deployable `jit/chaos/index.html` monolith**, but each capability retains a narrow contract, explicit state ownership, inspectability, deterministic behavior, and extraction readiness.
- Reuse the existing terrain, Hermeticon, entity contract, BB debris, material survey, gravity, contact, impact, and view machinery; inspect before modifying.
- The terrain is a **continuous volume** with a top, sides, and underside. Do not impose a generated illustration's invented thickness, wedge, caves, or symmetric cross-section on executable geometry.
- The three views must represent **one world from different orientations**, not three separate worlds or unrelated scenes.
- The Hermeticon is a spherical **viewport**; its equatorial ring is visual hardware, not a terrain boundary or material wall by implication.
- Preserve strong silhouettes, controlled visual density, and a mobile-first ~60 FPS target. Instrument actual counts, costs, and failure states.
- Distinguish **observed behavior**, **source-verified code**, and **aspirational concept**. Generated images are proposals, never authority.

## Visual character

**Crown:** branching, upward-growing, expansive, colorful, populated.

**Seam:** dense, structural, vascular, industrial, mechanically explicit.

**Underside:** suspended, articulated, translucent, luminous, spacious, mysterious.

Use brass and oxidized metal alongside ceramic, glass, stone, bone-like composites, dark mineral chassis, luminous BB fibers, and translucent membranes. The world should feel engineered by living things and alive through engineering.

**North-star sentence:** *One extraordinary, continuous, executable civilization revealing different aspects of itself depending on where we look.*
