STRAND

Four independent battlefield and terrain experiments. The 2D games use Canvas; Plateau Island uses native WebGL. These are not the Abyss Strand runtime.

01 · TOP-DOWN / EMERGENT PHYSICS

🌲 Pocket Battlefield

Agents, trees, fallen logs, rocks, persistent corpses and excavatable terrain.

02 · SIDE VIEW / CORPSE PHYSICS

💀 The Corpsefield

Enemies clamber over fallen bodies, corpse piles block arrows, and falls cause damage.

03 · ISLAND DEFENSE / TACTICAL ROGUELITE

⚔ Northfall

Three controllable companies defend five houses against six longship invasions. Terrain elevation, morale, stamina, abilities and between-wave upgrades.

04 · WEBGL / ORBITABLE 3D TERRAIN

⛰ Plateau Island

Three tiered plateaus with solid cliff walls, houses, trees, adjustable height, and an orbit camera. Self-contained WebGL.
