The Cartographer's Last Mistake

At the edge of every map, the cartographer drew a little door.

For years, no one noticed. They were too busy arguing over the roads, the borders, and which kingdom owned the river.

Then a child asked what lay behind the doors.

“Nothing,” said the cartographer. “They mark the places I have not been.”

“Then why,” asked the child, “do they all have handles on our side?”

The cartographer looked at his maps for a very long time.

And for the first time in his life, he stopped drawing.
