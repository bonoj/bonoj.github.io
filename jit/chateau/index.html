In 1885, architect Edmond Jacques Eckel completed a singular Chateauesque residence for Colonel Nathan Phipps Ogden on St. Joseph's storied Hall Street.

Its carved woodwork, original stained glass, richly layered interiors and rare panoramic wallpaper preserve the ambition of the Gilded Age with unusual completeness. Today, the Chateau is offered to a new steward.
